In secret — not in the open — bring your ship in
to your own dear country.

For there is no trusting women any more.

But come, tell me this, and tell it to me truly:

do you hear whether my son is still alive, somewhere —
in Orchomenus perhaps, or in sandy Pylos,
or with Menelaus in wide Sparta?

For noble Orestes has not yet died on the earth.'
So he spoke, and I answered him and said:

'Son of Atreus, why ask me this?

I know nothing —
whether he lives or is dead.

It is evil to speak words empty as wind.'
