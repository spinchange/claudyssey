delivery of the English hexameter. An acute accent marks each governing beat;
vertical bars divide the six feet. Hyphens clarify syllable boundaries,
especially where a foot divides a word. Unmarked syllables may still take
secondary stress in natural speech. A shortened foot is intentional, not a
missing beat.

The marks describe **accentual English** meter. They do not assign Greek-style
quantity to English vowels.
