Come now, tell me these things, and relate every matter with truth here:

where have you wandered and into what countries of mankind have you entered?

Tell of the people and cities where excellent houses are standing,
both of the violent, savage, and those who are strangers to justice,
also the friendly to strangers, with god-fearing hearts in their bodies.

Tell me the cause why you weep and lament in the heart you are bearing,
hearing the fate of the Argive Danaans and city of Ilium.

Gods made that destiny happen and spun the destruction for mortals,
so that among generations to come there might always be singing.

Did some relative dear to you die at the walls of the Trojans,
excellent man, some father-in-law or the husband of daughter,
those who are closest to us after those of our blood and our lineage?

Or did a companion die there, a man who was dear to your own heart,
excellent man, since never is less than the love of a brother
love for a comrade who carries clear thought and a true understanding?”
