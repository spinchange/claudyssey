There on the shore I presented the ram to the black-clouded great Zeus,
child of great Cronos and lord over all, burning thighs as an offering.

Yet he regarded it not, for already he pondered how all my
well-benched vessels should perish together with faithful companions.

So then all day long till the sun and the light were descending,
there we sat feasting on meat past counting and sweet wine beside it.

Then the sun entered the west and the darkness descended around us;

there we lay down to sleep on the surf-line there at the seashore.

When early Dawn had appeared with her beautiful rose-colored fingers,
then I aroused my companions and gave them commandment to enter
into the ships in their turn and release all the stern ropes behind them.

Straightway the men came aboard and sat at the oarlocks in order;

sitting in orderly fashion, they struck the gray sea with the oar-blades.

There we continued our sailing away with the heart full of sorrow,
glad to be free from death though our well-loved comrades had perished.
