Thus did I speak, and their own hearts broke in their bodies completely.

Right where they stood, they sat on the earth, pulled hair from their temples,
weeping;

but nothing of profit was born of their lamentation.

While we were going our way to the swift black ship and the seashore,
grieving deeply in heart and letting the great tears fall from us,
Circe meanwhile had gone in advance and beside the black vessel
tethered a dark ewe there with a ram for the rites of the dead men,
slipping with ease from our notice. For who with his eyes can behold one
god in despite of that god as the deathless one passes among us?
