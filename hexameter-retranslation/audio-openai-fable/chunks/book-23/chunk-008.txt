But now, since at last we both have attained our long-desired bed here,
all the possessions I have, you must tend them within these halls for me;

and the sheep that the proud suitors slaughtered and wasted for me,
many indeed I myself shall win back by foray and raiding,
and the Achaeans will give me the rest, till they fill every sheepfold.

But now to the field rich in trees I shall go on my journey,
so I may see my noble father, who sorely is grieving within him.

And on you, my wife, I lay this charge, though prudent you are fully:

straight with the sunrise the rumor will spread among men all together,
of the suitor-men whom I killed here within these halls of my household.

Go to the upper room and sit there with all your serving women;

look upon no one at all, and do not inquire and ask anything.”
So he spoke, and over his shoulders he put on the splendid armor,
and roused Telemachus, and the neat-herd, and also the swineherd,
and bade them all to take in their hands the weapons of battle.

They did not disobey him, but clothed themselves round in bronze armor,
opened the doors, and went on their way;

and Odysseus led them.

Now already the light spread over the earth, but Athena
wrapped them in night and swiftly led them out of the city.
