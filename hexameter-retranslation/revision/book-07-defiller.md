# Book 07 de-filler — all 16 filler-final lines reworked

Rules applied as in the Book 21 pilot: every replacement is a six-beat falling
line; no line ends in *there/now/then* (none needed a Greek warrant in this
book); the preferred fix is reordering so a naturally trochaic word lands last;
masculine endings budgeted at 3 per book — none were needed. A junior draft was
verified line by line; ten of its sixteen entries were rewritten in review,
mostly for joins with neighboring verses and for rising (iambic) movement.

---

**07.51** τάρβει· θαρσαλέος γὰρ ἀνὴρ ἐν πᾶσιν ἀμείνων
- OLD: always is best in the end in whatever endeavor he makes there,
- NEW: always is best in the end in whatever endeavor he ventures,
- Athena's dialogue; no contractions.

**07.57** γείνατο καὶ Περίβοια, γυναικῶν εἶδος ἀρίστη,
- OLD: and Periboea, most beautiful woman of all then,
- NEW: and Periboea, in beauty of form the foremost of women,
- "in beauty of form" unpacks εἶδος; "among women" was avoided because 58 opens
  "youngest among all the daughters" and would double "among."

**07.100** χρύσειοι δʼ ἄρα κοῦροι ἐυδμήτων ἐπὶ βωμῶν
- OLD: Golden young men upon strongly constructed pedestals stood there,
- NEW: Golden young men stood there upon strongly constructed pedestals,
- Pure reorder; "there" is retained mid-line, where it is permitted.

**07.107** καιρουσσέων δʼ ὀθονέων ἀπολείβεται ὑγρὸν ἔλαιον.
- OLD: oil drips softly and smooth from the linen that closely they weave there.
- NEW: oil drips softly and smooth from the linens closely-woven.
- καιρουσσέων ὀθονέων is "close-woven linens"; the old relative clause
  ("that... they weave") was not in the Greek, and the plural is restored.

**07.124** τέρσεται ἠελίῳ, ἑτέρας δʼ ἄρα τε τρυγόωσιν,
- OLD: baking beneath the sun; other grapes they are gathering in there,
- NEW: baking beneath the sun; other grapes they are gathering,
- "harvesting" was rejected: 123 ends "where the harvest is drying," and 125
  carries "while"; the minimal cut keeps clear of both neighbors.

**07.128** παντοῖαι πεφύασιν, ἐπηετανὸν γανόωσαι·
- OLD: beds of every green that are shining throughout the full year there.
- NEW: beds of every green, and throughout the full year they are shining.
- γανόωσαι, the participle, now closes the line.

**07.140** πολλὴν ἠέρʼ ἔχων, ἥν οἱ περίχευεν Ἀθήνη,
- OLD: clothed in the thickening mist that the goddess Athena had poured there,
- NEW: clothed in the thickening mist that around him was poured by Athena,
- περί ("around") and οἱ ("him") are now rendered; the name closes the line as
  it does the Greek.

**07.182** ὣς φάτο, Ποντόνοος δὲ μελίφρονα οἶνον ἐκίρνα,
- OLD: Thus did he speak, and Pontonous mixed up the heartening wine there,
- NEW: Thus did he speak, and the heartening wine Pontonous was mixing,
- Object-fronted rather than passive: 183's "serving them all..." must attach
  to Pontonous, so he stays the grammatical subject. "was mixing" matches the
  imperfect ἐκίρνα.

**07.224** καί περ πολλὰ παθόντα· ἰδόντα με καὶ λίποι αἰὼν
- OLD: even though greatly afflicted; and may I die when I have seen there
- NEW: even though greatly afflicted; and may life leave me on seeing
- λίποι αἰών is literally "may life leave me"; "on seeing" keeps 225's list
  ("all my possessions and slaves...") governed by the verb of seeing.

**07.230** αὐτὰρ ὁ ἐν μεγάρῳ ὑπελείπετο δῖος Ὀδυσσεύς,
- OLD: leaving the noble Odysseus behind in the hall by himself there.
- NEW: leaving behind in the hall by himself the noble Odysseus.
- Reorder ending on the name; a mid-line "there" was avoided because 231 opens
  "Queen Arete remained there beside him."

**07.268** ὀκτωκαιδεκάτῃ δʼ ἐφάνη ὄρεα σκιόεντα
- OLD: then on the eighteenth day all the shadowy mountains appeared there,
- NEW: then on the eighteenth day the shadowy mountains were sighted,
- Ending on "mountains" would double 269's opening word; "were sighted"
  restates ἐφάνη, and the un-Greek "all" is dropped.

**07.282** λεῖος πετράων, καὶ ἐπὶ σκέπας ἦν ἀνέμοιο.
- OLD: clear of all stones, and a place of protection existed from wind there.
- NEW: clear of all stones, and a shelter against the wind was provided.
- "provided" is a mild addition beyond the bare ἦν, logged here; the
  existential "there was" was dropped because 283 opens "There I collapsed."

**07.295** ἥ μοι σῖτον ἔδωκεν ἅλις ἠδʼ αἴθοπα οἶνον
- OLD: Food she provided in plentiful measure and fire-bright wine there,
- NEW: Fire-bright wine she provided, and food in plentiful measure,
- Food and wine trade places for the cadence; "gave/giving" was avoided
  because 296 carries "she gave me the clothing."

**07.305** ἀλλʼ ἐγὼ οὐκ ἔθελον δείσας αἰσχυνόμενός τε,
- OLD: I was unwilling because of my fear and a sense of the shame there,
- NEW: But I myself was unwilling to do so, ashamed and frightened,
- ἀλλ' ἐγώ regains "But I myself"; "to do so" makes the elided infinitive
  explicit (logged). The participles are swapped so "frightened" sits beside
  the "lest..." clause of 306 that the fear governs.

**07.326** ἤματι τῷ αὐτῷ καὶ ἀπήνυσαν οἴκαδʼ ὀπίσσω.
- OLD: sailing again and completing the homeward return the same day there.
- NEW: sailing again on that same day, they completed the passage homeward.
- The "-ward" compound closes the line, in the poem's idiom.

**07.332** Ἀλκίνοος· τοῦ μέν κεν ἐπὶ ζείδωρον ἄρουραν
- OLD: then on the grain-giving earth his fame shall never be quenched there,
- NEW: never upon the grain-giving earth shall his fame be extinguished,
- The predicate belongs to the next Greek verse's ἄσβεστον κλέος
  ("unquenchable fame"): "fame" is kept, and "extinguished" renders the
  quenching image with a falling close. Odysseus's prayer; mortal speaker.

---

## Tally

- 16 filler-final lines reworked; 0 now end in unmotivated *there/now/then*,
  and none required a Greek warrant.
- 0 masculine endings used (budget of 3 untouched).
- Fidelity recovered along the way: the plural ὀθονέων at 107, περί + οἱ at
  140, the imperfect ἐκίρνα at 182, λίποι αἰών at 224, ἀλλ' ἐγώ at 305; the
  un-Greek "all" cut at 268, the un-Greek relative clause cut at 107.
- Honest costs incurred: "provided" at 282 and "to do so" at 305 (both
  logged); food/wine order swapped at 295; the fear/shame participles
  swapped at 305.
- Out of scope but noted: seven verses in this book end in "here" (23, 25,
  179, 187, 214, 243, 307), a class the extraction did not target.
