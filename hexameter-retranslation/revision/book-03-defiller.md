# Book 03 de-filler — all 10 filler-final lines reworked

Rules applied as in the Book 21 pilot: every replacement is a six-beat falling
line; no line ends in unmotivated *there/now/then*; the preferred fix is
reordering onto a naturally trochaic close (participles, names, plurals,
compounds); no masculine ending was needed in this set. Junior draft verified
line by line; 8 of 10 entries were rewritten in review (scan failures,
dropped Greek, or clashes with a neighboring verse), 2 accepted as drafted.

---

**03.11** στεῖλαν ἀείραντες, τὴν δʼ ὥρμισαν, ἐκ δʼ ἔβαν αὐτοί·
- OLD: sails and furled them above, moored the ship, and themselves disembarked there.
- NEW: sails and furled them above; and they moored her, themselves disembarking.
- αὐτοί ("they themselves") kept, which the draft had dropped; "her" for τὴν
  (the vessel of line 10) also clears the duplication with 12's "left the ship."

**03.64** ὣς δʼ αὔτως ἠρᾶτο Ὀδυσσῆος φίλος υἱός.
- OLD: just as the goddess had prayed, the dear son of Odysseus prayed then.
- NEW: just as the goddess had prayed, so too prayed the dear son of Odysseus.
- ὣς δʼ αὔτως is "just so," not temporal; the name takes the feminine close.

**03.78** ἠδʼ ἵνα μιν κλέος ἐσθλὸν ἐν ἀνθρώποισιν ἔχῃσιν·
- OLD: also that noble renown among mortals might come to himself there:
- NEW: also that noble renown might be his to hold among mortals:
- ἔχῃσιν ("might hold") rendered directly; unstressed-final plural closes.

**03.258** τῶ κέ οἱ οὐδὲ θανόντι χυτὴν ἐπὶ γαῖαν ἔχευαν,
- OLD: No man would heap up a mound of the earth on the dead man's remains then;
- NEW: So not even a mound of earth would they have poured for the fallen;
- τῶ = "So"; ἔχευαν restored to its plural indefinite subject (as in Murray);
  χυτήν is carried by "poured," so the draft's "heaped-up" (a seventh beat) went.
  θανόντι + οἱ close the line as "the fallen."

**03.311** αὐτῆμαρ δέ οἱ ἦλθε βοὴν ἀγαθὸς Μενέλαος
- OLD: That very day Menelaus, renowned for the war cry, arrived there,
- NEW: That same day there came to him Menelaus, renowned for the war cry,
- οἱ ("to him," Orestes) restored; "there came" is expletive, rendering ἦλθε,
  not the excised locative. The epithet keeps the book's established order
  ("Menelaus, renowned for the war cry"), and the compound "war cry" gives the
  falling close; the draft's name-final inversion ran to eighteen syllables.

**03.374** Τηλεμάχου δʼ ἕλε χεῖρα, ἔπος τʼ ἔφατʼ ἔκ τʼ ὀνόμαζεν·
- OLD: Taking Telemachus' hand, he addressed him aloud by his name there:
- NEW: Taking Telemachus' hand, he spoke, and by name he addressed him:
- The full formula (ἔπος τʼ ἔφατʼ / ἔκ τʼ ὀνόμαζεν = spoke / called by name),
  keeping the book's own verb "addressed" and closing feminine on "addressed him."

**03.380** ἀλλὰ ἄνασσʼ ἵληθι, δίδωθι δέ μοι κλέος ἐσθλόν,
- OLD: Queen, be propitious, and grant excellent glory and fame now,
- NEW: Queen, be propitious, and grant excellent fame and glory,
- The two nouns (the old line's doubling of κλέος ἐσθλόν) reordered so the
  trochaic "glory" closes. The draft's restored "to me" was rejected: μοι is
  already carried by 381's "both upon me," which it would have duplicated.

**03.408** λευκοί, ἀποστίλβοντες ἀλείφατος· οἷς ἔπι μὲν πρὶν
- OLD: white-colored stones, all shining as though they were polished with oil there.
- NEW: white-colored stones, as though they were polished with oil, all shining.
- Pure reorder: the appositive to 406's "stone seats" is preserved and the
  participle ἀποστίλβοντες ("shining") closes. οἷς ἔπι μὲν πρίν remains covered
  by 409's "There in the days long ago."

**03.455** τῆς δʼ ἐπεὶ ἐκ μέλαν αἷμα ῥύη, λίπε δʼ ὀστέα θυμός,
- OLD: Then, when the black blood ran and the life left all of the bones there,
- NEW: Then, when the black blood ran out of her, and the bones lost their spirit,
- τῆς ἐκ restored ("out of her," the heifer); the old line's "all of" was
  padding. λίπε δʼ ὀστέα θυμός is voice-flipped ("the bones lost their spirit"
  for "the spirit left the bones") to land the trochaic θυμός-word last — the
  draft's close, "the bones of her body," collided with 456's "the body."

**03.459** καῖε δʼ ἐπὶ σχίζῃς ὁ γέρων, ἐπὶ δʼ αἴθοπα οἶνον
- OLD: Nestor burned these on split pieces of wood, pouring fire-colored wine there;
- NEW: Nestor burned these on split wood, the fire-colored wine on them pouring;
- "on them" renders the second ἐπί (which the old line's "there" was gesturing
  at); participle closes. The book's epithet "fire-colored" for αἴθοπα is kept —
  the draft's silent switch to "fire-bright" was reverted.

---

## Tally

- 10 filler-final lines reworked; 0 now end in unmotivated *there/now/then*.
- 0 masculine endings used (budget of 3 untouched).
- 0 warranted locative/temporal closes retained: no αὐτόθι/ἔνθα/τότε sat
  line-final in the Greek of this set.
- Fidelity recovered along the way: αὐτοί at 11, ἔχῃσιν at 78, the plural
  subject of ἔχευαν at 258, οἱ at 311, the full naming formula at 374,
  τῆς ἐκ at 455, the second ἐπί at 459; padding cut at 258 ("of the earth"
  doubling), 455 ("all of").
- Out of scope, flagged for a future pass: four verses end in "here"
  (03.15, 03.50, 03.61, 03.351) — "here" was outside this extraction's
  there/now/then pattern; and the book's pre-existing text has a few
  masculine closes of its own (e.g. 03.296 "back," 03.336 "Let us go,"
  03.421 "as he can go"), untouched under the only-listed-verses rule.
