**08.28** keeps its “here”: ἵκετʼ ἐμὸν δῶ with deictic ὅδε — “this stranger has come to my house here.”

Speech-formula renderings now used in this book, each fixed and distinct:

- finite ὣς φάτο / ὣς ἔφατ(ο) / ὣς φάθ᾿ / ὣς ἄρ᾿ ἔφη / ὣς φάν → **“So he spoke,” / “So she spoke,” / “So they spoke,”**
- participial ὣς εἰπών / ὣς εἰποῦσ᾿ / ὣς ἄρα φωνήσας → **“So having spoken,”**
- ἦ ῥα καί → **“He spoke, and …”** (deliberately *not* the ruled formula)
- iterative ὧδε δέ τις εἴπεσκεν (8.328) → **“So one god would … say”** — already in the ruled iterative shape; left as it stood.
