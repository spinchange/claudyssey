# Book 20 "Then"-opening revision

**Examined:** 38 verse-initial "Then/then" openings. **Kept (warranted):** 3. **Rewritten:** 35.

**Warrant rule:** a verse-initial "Then" survives only where Murray's Greek carries a real
temporal adverb at that joint — τότε, δὴ τότε, καὶ τότε, ἔπειτα, αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα, temporal
ἔνθα, ἦμος/εὖτε…τόφρα correlatives. Where the Greek has only a light connective (bare δέ,
δ᾽ ἄρα, δ᾽ αὖτε, αὐτάρ/ἀτάρ, καί, γάρ, ὥς + verb, participial linkage, asyndeton), the
"Then" is a translator's tic and was replaced with an opening that reflects the actual
connective. All replacements hold the six-beat falling line; no rewritten line ends in
*there/now/then/here*; no single replacement opening exceeds a fifth of the rewrites.

Verbatim Greek formulas were re-unified in English (pilot rule 5): τὸν/τὴν δ᾽
ἀπαμειβόμενος προσέφη πολύμητις Ὀδυσσεύς → "Answering him/her in return spoke resourceful
Odysseus, saying:" (20.36, 168, 226); τὸν δ᾽ αὖτε προσέειπε → "To him in turn made
answer…" (20.44, 134, 235, 363); αὐτὸς δ᾽ αὖτ᾽ … προσηύδα → "and in his turn addressed
Odysseus with … words, saying:" (20.165, 177).

## Kept as warranted (residual "then"-openings: 3)

- **20.77** — τόφρα δέ: true temporal correlative answering εὖτε (20.73), "in that very time."
- **20.209** — ὤ μοι ἔπειτ᾽: ἔπειτα on the warrant list (apodotic "then, in that case").
- **20.237** — γνοίης χ᾽ (κε + optative): conditional apodosis of 20.236's wish — a load-bearing
  if-then, not a temporal connective tic.

No crowding among the keeps (77 / 209 / 237 are each isolated).
