# Book 19 "Then"-opening revision — 44 examined, 3 kept, 41 rewritten

*(Second pass, 2026-08-23: the one borderline keep flagged below, 19.310 τῷ κε,
has now been rewritten under the strict temporal-adverb warrant list; counts
updated. All other entries are from the first pass and unchanged.)*

Warrant rule: a line-initial "Then" survives only where the Greek carries a real
temporal adverb (τότε, δὴ τότε, καὶ τότε, ἔπειτα, αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα, temporal ἔνθα,
ἦμος-clauses, apodotic τῷ). Where the Greek opens with only a light connective
(bare δέ, αὐτάρ, μὲν ἄρα, ῥα, καί, or asyndeton), the "Then" was a translator's
metronome and has been removed. Replacement openings are keyed to the actual
Greek connective; no single opening word exceeds 4 of the 40 rewrites (10%).
Every rewritten line still scans as six falling beats; no edited line ends in
filler *there/now/then/here*; verse count unchanged (604).

Where the Greek repeats a formula verbatim and the old English matched, the new
English matches too (164 = 220; 499 = 554; 414 = 459 τὸν μὲν ἄρʼ Αὐτόλυκός τε;
103 ≈ 508 τοῖσι δὲ μύθων ἦρχε, same structure with each line's own epithet).

## Kept as warranted (3)

- **19.35** — δὴ τότε ("then indeed"): Then Telemachus quickly addressed his father
  (αἶψα also present, already rendered "quickly").
- **19.149** — ἔνθα, temporal, inside Penelope's web narrative: Then by day I would weave.
- **19.433** — Ἠέλιος μὲν ἔπειτα: Then did the Sun strike fresh.

No crowding among the keeps: no two surviving "Then"-openings fall within 10 lines
of each other.

## For human review

- 19.123 (τὸν δʼ ἠμείβετʼ ἔπειτα): the formula literally contains ἔπειτα, but the
  project's speech-reply rule treats the formula as a whole; the "Then" was removed.
  Reverse if the editors prefer the letter of the ἔπειτα warrant.
- 19.310 (τῷ κε): the first pass kept it although τῷ is not on the explicit
  warrant list, reading it as a true apodotic "then." RESOLVED in the second
  pass: rewritten to "So would you swiftly know…" under the strict list; "So"
  still carries the apodotic force. Revert if the editors prefer the first
  pass's reading.
- The nine-fold Greek formula τὴν/τὸν δʼ ἀπαμειβόμενος προσέφη πολύμητις Ὀδυσσεύς
  is not rendered uniformly in English (the pre-existing text already varied it);
  uniformity here would have re-created a metronome, so variation was kept, with
  exact English matches preserved where the old text had them (164=220, 499=554).
