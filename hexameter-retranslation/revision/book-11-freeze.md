# Book 11 freeze — speech-formula standardization + here-final sweep

Verses changed: **22** (19 by the ὣς φάτο ruling, 3 by the here-final sweep).
Scansion lines updated in `book-11-performance.md`: **22**.
Verse count unchanged against the Greek (640 lines).

Retained line-initial *Thus*, each Greek-warranted:

- **11.150** ὣς φαμένη — participial, distinct from the finite family; “Thus having spoken” is the poem’s dominant participial rendering (22 occurrences corpus-wide).
- **11.253**, **11.627** ὣς εἰπών — same participial formula, same rendering.
- **11.412** ὣς θάνον — not a speech formula; ὣς here genuinely means “in this way (I died).”

Retained filler-looking close, Greek-warranted:

- **11.372** “…encountered their death there” — αὐτοῦ πότμον ἐπέσπον: αὐτοῦ is a true locative.
