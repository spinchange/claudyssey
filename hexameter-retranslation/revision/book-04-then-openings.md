# Book 4 "Then"-opening revision

Examined 33 verses opening with "Then" (including after an opening quotation mark).
**Kept 5** warranted; **rewrote 28** habitual.

Warrant rule: a "Then"-opening stands only where the Greek carries a real temporal
adverb — τότε, δὴ τότε, καὶ τότε, ἔπειτα, αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα, temporal ἔνθα, ἦμος-clauses.
Light connectives (bare δέ, αὐτάρ/ἀτάρ, ὣς + verb, καί, γάρ, τε, asyndeton) do not
warrant it; those lines were reopened to reflect their actual connective. Speech-reply
formulas (τὸν/τὴν δ᾿ ἀπαμειβόμενος προσέφη, τὸν δ᾿ ἠμείβετ᾿ ἔπειτα, τὸν δ᾿ αὖ …
ἀντίον ηὔδα) became answer-formulas without "Then" even where ἔπειτα rides inside
the formula (04.808), since the formula, not time, is what the Greek is doing.
Where the Greek repeats a formula verbatim, the English replacement repeats verbatim
(147=265; 290=315=593; 383=399; 428≈573; 824=835). Every replacement still scans as
six falling beats; no rewritten line ends in filler; no replacement opening exceeds
4 of 28 (14%).
