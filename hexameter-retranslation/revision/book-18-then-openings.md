# Book 18 — “Then”-opening revision

First pass — examined: **41** verses opening with “Then.” Kept (warranted): **5**. Rewritten (habitual): **36**.

Second pass (2026-08-23) — swept the residue, this time counting lowercase
verse-initial “then” as well: examined **10** (the 5 capital keeps plus 5
lowercase openings at 60, 152, 379, 385, 428). Kept **7**, rewrote **3**
(152, 250, 385 — see the second-pass section at the end). Residual
“Then/then”-openings in the book: **7**, all warranted.

Warrant rule: a “Then” opening stands only where the Greek carries a real temporal
adverb — τότε, δὴ τότε, καὶ τότε, ἔπειτα, αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα, temporal ἔνθα, ἦμος-clauses.
Lines whose Greek offers only a light connective (bare δέ, δʼ ἄρα, αὖτε, καί,
asyndeton) lose the “Then” and open instead off their actual connective and word
order — subject-first, verb-first, prepositional, participial. No replacement
opening exceeds four uses in the book (And ×4, But ×4); all edited lines still
scan as six falling beats, none ends in filler.

## Second pass — 3 further rewrites

**18.152** ἂψ δʼ ἐν χερσὶν ἔθηκε δέπας κοσμήτορι λαῶν — bare δέ (ἄψ “back” is
already carried by “once more”; the lowercase “then” had no Greek warrant)
- OLD: then placed the cup once more in the hands of the marshal of people.
- NEW: and placed the cup once more in the hands of the marshal of people.

**18.250** τὸν δʼ ἠμείβετʼ ἔπειτα περίφρων Πηνελόπεια — speech-reply formula.
The first pass kept this on its ἔπειτα, but the ἔπειτα inside the fixed reply
tag τὸν δʼ ἠμείβετʼ ἔπειτα is bleached — reply formulas drop the “Then” by
rule. Rendered as an answer-formula distinct from the book’s “Making reply …”
line (which maps τὸν δʼ ἀπαμειβόμενος προσέφη at 124/365).
- OLD: Then in return to him prudent Penelope answered:
- NEW: Making her answer, prudent Penelope spoke and addressed him:

**18.385** αἶψά κέ τοι τὰ θύρετρα, καὶ εὐρέα περ μάλʼ ἐόντα — αἶψα, fronted in
the Greek; the old line spent it at line-end as “all too quickly” and opened on
an unwarranted “then.” The rewrite restores αἶψα to its Greek position
(“swiftly”) and recovers μάλα (“very”); the conditional apodosis stays carried
by 386’s “would.” Also relieves the then-cluster at 375 (mid-line) / 379 / 385.
- OLD: then for you those doors, though indeed they are broad, all too quickly
- NEW: swiftly for you those doors, though indeed they are very broad ones,
