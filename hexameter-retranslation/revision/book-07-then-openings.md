# Book 07 "Then"-opening revision — 28 examined, 3 kept, 25 rewritten

Warrant rule: a "Then"-opening stands only where Murray's Greek carries a real
temporal adverb at the head of the sense unit — τότε, δὴ τότε, καὶ τότε, ἔπειτα,
αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα, temporal ἔνθα, or an ἦμος-then apodosis. A "Then" riding on a light
connective — bare δέ, αὐτάρ/ἀτάρ, ἀλλά, ὣς + verb, ἦ ῥα, καί, γάρ, τε, or
asyndeton — is habitual and was rewritten to reflect the actual connective.
Every rewrite still scans as six falling beats; no rewritten line ends in
*there/now/then/here*; no new "Then"-openings introduced; verse count 347
unchanged. Recurring Greek formulas were given one recurring English rendering
(207 = 302, and 240 with her/him varied; 184 = 228 verbatim; 298 = 308 verbatim).

**07.3** ἡ δʼ ὅτε δή (light δέ + when-clause)
- OLD: Then, when she came to the glorious palace belonging to father,
- NEW: When she arrived at the glorious palace belonging to father,

**07.27** τὸν δʼ αὖτε προσέειπε (reply formula, bare δέ + αὖτε "in turn")
- OLD: Then the gray-eyed goddess Athena addressed him in answer:
- NEW: Speaking in turn, the gray-eyed goddess Athena addressed him:

**07.46** ἀλλʼ ὅτε δή (ἀλλά)
- OLD: Then, when they came to the glorious house of Alcinous the ruler,
- NEW: Yet when they came to the glorious house of Alcinous the ruler,
- Matches the poem's own render of ἀλλʼ ὅτε δή at 7.18 ("Yet when Odysseus…").

**07.61** τῇ δέ (bare δέ, anaphoric pronoun)

- NEW: With that maiden Poseidon lay and fathered a great son,

**07.63** Ναυσίθοος δʼ (subject + bare δέ)

- NEW: And Nausithous fathered Alcinous and lord Rhexenor;
