# Book 22 "Then"-opening pass — 58 examined, 8 kept, 50 rewritten

(Second sweep 2026-08-23: the borderline keep 22.134 flagged below was
re-audited under the strict temporal-only warrant standard and rewritten;
entry added in sequence, keep-list and notes updated. All other findings of
the first pass re-verified against the Greek and confirmed.)

Warrant rule: a line may open with "Then" only where the Greek carries a real
temporal adverb (τότε, δὴ τότε, καὶ τότε, ἔπειτα, αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα, temporal ἔνθα,
ἦμος-clauses). Light connectives — bare δέ, αὐτάρ/ἀτάρ, δ᾿ ἄρα, δ᾿ αὖτε, ἦ τοι,
καί, γάρ, τε, μέν, asyndeton — lose the "Then". Rewrites reflect the actual
connective: αὐτάρ → "But"; δέ → light "And", subject-first, or verb-first;
αὖτε/αὖτις → "in turn"/"again"; ἀπαμειβόμενος → the answering-formula. Every
replacement line still scans as six falling beats. No replacement opening
exceeds a fifth of the rewrites ("And" 8, "But" 8, each 16%).

Formula normalizations made along the way (Greek lines verbatim-identical):
the ὑπόδρα-ἰδών glare (34 = 60 = 320, now "Glowering darkly upon
them/him, spoke out resourceful Odysseus"), the ἀπαμειβόμενος reply
(105 = 170 = 430 = 490, "Answering him/her in turn…"), the Eurycleia reply
(419 = 485), the Agelaus speech-intro (131 = 247), and the rope-line
(175 = 192). 320, 430, and 490 previously rendered πολύμητις as "wise"/"of the
many devices"; they now use the poem's usual "resourceful". 480 carried a stray
trailing opening-quote in the old text; it is removed with the edit.

Crowding: after the pass no window of ~10 lines holds more than two
"Then"-openings; the closest surviving pair is 390/401, eleven lines apart
(the worst pre-pass cluster, 131/134/135, now keeps none).

Note on 22.134: kept on the first pass as an apodotic "then" (τῷ κε is a real
anaphoric adverb, "in that case"), rewritten on the second sweep under the
strict temporal-only standard. Residual for human review: Greek 134 = Greek 78
verbatim, but English 78 splits the clause mid-line ("…Then that man would
have shot his last arrow.") because βοὴ δ᾿ ὤκιστα γένοιτο spills into it from
77 — the two renderings cannot echo verbatim without recasting 77–78; a later
formula pass could harmonize the shared half ("shot his last").
