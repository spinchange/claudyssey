# Book 24 "Then"-opening revision — 51 of 60 rewritten

Warrant rule: a verse-initial "Then" survives only when the Greek carries a real
temporal adverb at that position — τότε, δὴ τότε, καὶ τότε, ἔπειτα, αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα,
temporal ἔνθα, or a when/then apodosis marker (plus, this book, one conditional
apodosis τῷ κεν, the logical "in that case" of a counterfactual wish). A "Then"
translating only a light connective — bare δέ, αὐτάρ, καί, ὣς + verb, ἦ ῥα, τε,
or asyndeton — is habitual and was rewritten. Where 3+ "Then"-openings crowded
within ~10 lines, the least emphatic was varied even if warranted (one case: 139).

Counts: 60 examined · 9 kept warranted · 51 rewritten · residual "Then/then"-openings
after edit: 9 (the keeps). Verse count 548, unchanged. Every replacement line still
scans as six falling beats; no edited line ends in *there/now/then/here*; no new
"Then"-openings introduced; the openers used for rewrites are spread so that no
single choice exceeds one-fifth (largest family: "And/and" ×6, "Answering" ×5,
"Him" ×5 of 51).

Formula repairs made in passing (Greek-verbatim repetitions now repeat in English):
35 = 191 (τὸν δ᾽ αὖτε ψυχὴ προσεφώνεεν Ἀτρεΐδαο — old 191 had "spirit" against
35's "soul"); 302 = 330 = 356 = 406 (τὸν δ᾽ ἀπαμειβόμενος προσέφη πολύμητις
Ὀδυσσεύς — old had four different renderings), with 477 as its τὴν δ᾽ mirror;
372 = 399 (καί μιν φωνήσας ἔπεα πτερόεντα προσηύδα); 385 ≈ 411 (ἑξείης ἕζοντο →
"in due order"). At 327 the old "wise" was dropped: Greek 327 has no πεπνυμένος
(it does at 375 and 510, where "wise" stays).
