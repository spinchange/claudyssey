# Book 01 — "Then"-opening pass

Examined 20 verse-initial "Then"/"then" openings. **Kept 4** (Greek carries a real
temporal adverb: τότε, δὴ τότε, ἔπειτα, temporal ἔνθα). **Rewrote 16** where the Greek
has only a light connective (bare δέ, δ᾽ αὖ/αὖτε reply-formulas, καί, ἦ ῥα-type verb
openings, τῷ κεν) — those "Then"s were translator habit, not Homer. Every replacement
line still scans as six falling beats; openings are varied (no replacement opening
exceeds ~1/5 of the rewrites; the ἠμείβετ᾽-ἔπειτα Athena formula recurs verbatim in the
Greek at 44 = 80 = 314, so its rendering recurs verbatim too — 3 of 16, at the cap).

---

**01.44** τὸν δ᾽ ἠμείβετ᾽ ἔπειτα (reply formula; ἔπειτα is formulaic, not emphatic)
- OLD: Then in reply spoke the gray-eyed goddess Athena directly:
- NEW: Speaking in answer, the gray-eyed goddess Athena addressed him:

**01.80** τὸν δ᾽ ἠμείβετ᾽ ἔπειτα (same formula as 44)
- OLD: Then in reply spoke the gray-eyed goddess Athena directly:
- NEW: Speaking in answer, the gray-eyed goddess Athena addressed him:

**01.93** πέμψω δ᾽ (bare δέ; verb-first in the Greek)
- OLD: Then I shall send him to Sparta and onward to sand-covered Pylos,
- NEW: Him I shall send to Sparta and onward to sand-covered Pylos,

**01.122** καί μιν φωνήσας (plain καί)
- OLD: then he addressed her aloud, and his words had the swiftness of wingbeats:
- NEW: and he addressed her aloud, and his words had the swiftness of wingbeats:

**01.213** τὴν δ᾽ αὖ … ἀντίον ηὔδα (reply formula, δ᾽ αὖ)
- OLD: Then in reply clear-minded Telemachus spoke and addressed her:
- NEW: And in reply clear-minded Telemachus spoke and addressed her:

**01.230** τὴν δ᾽ αὖ … ἀντίον ηὔδα (reply formula, δ᾽ αὖ)
- OLD: Then in reply clear-minded Telemachus spoke and addressed her:
- NEW: Turning to face her, clear-minded Telemachus uttered his answer:

**01.239** τῷ κέν (inferential "in that case," not a temporal adverb)
- OLD: Then the Achaeans together would raise him a tomb in his honor,
- NEW: So would the gathered Achaeans have raised him a tomb in his honor,

**01.288** ἦ τ᾽ ἄν (asseverative particle only; concessive περ kept as "even")
- OLD: then, though distressed and diminished, endure for another whole year still.
- NEW: even distressed and diminished, endure for another whole year still.

**01.306** τὴν δ᾽ αὖ … ἀντίον ηὔδα (reply formula, δ᾽ αὖ "once more")
- OLD: Then in reply clear-minded Telemachus spoke and addressed her:
- NEW: Once more in answer clear-minded Telemachus spoke and addressed her:

**01.314** τὸν δ᾽ ἠμείβετ᾽ ἔπειτα (same formula as 44, 80)
- OLD: Then in reply spoke the gray-eyed goddess Athena directly:
- NEW: Speaking in answer, the gray-eyed goddess Athena addressed him:

**01.345** τὴν δ᾽ αὖ … ἀντίον ηὔδα (reply formula, δ᾽ αὖ; addressed to Penelope)
- OLD: Then in reply clear-minded Telemachus spoke and addressed her:
- NEW: Facing his mother, clear-minded Telemachus made her an answer:

**01.367** τοῖσι δὲ … ἤρχετο μύθων (bare δέ)
- OLD: Then clear-minded Telemachus started a speech to the suitors:
- NEW: Facing the suitors, clear-minded Telemachus opened his speaking:

**01.383** τὸν δ᾽ αὖτ᾽ … προσέφη (reply formula, δ᾽ αὖτε "in turn" → "Next")
- OLD: Then in reply came Antinous, son of Eupeithes, and spoke thus:
- NEW: Next in reply came Antinous, son of Eupeithes, and spoke thus:

**01.388** τὸν δ᾽ αὖ … ἀντίον ηὔδα (reply formula, δ᾽ αὖ)
- OLD: Then in reply clear-minded Telemachus spoke and addressed him:
- NEW: And in reply clear-minded Telemachus spoke and addressed him:

**01.412** τὸν δ᾽ αὖ … ἀντίον ηὔδα (reply formula, δ᾽ αὖ)
- OLD: Then in reply clear-minded Telemachus spoke and addressed him:
- NEW: Turning to face him, clear-minded Telemachus uttered his answer:

**01.441** βῆ ῥ᾽ ἴμεν ἐκ θαλάμοιο (verb + ῥα, light)
- OLD: then from the chamber went out, pulling shut by the silver door handle
- NEW: out of the chamber she went, pulling shut by the silver door handle
