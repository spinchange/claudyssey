# Book 12 freeze — speech-formula standardization + here-final sweep

Verses changed: **12** (11 by the ὣς φάτο ruling, 1 by the here-final sweep).
Scansion lines updated in `book-12-performance.md`: **12**.
Verse count unchanged against the Greek (453 lines).

No line-initial *Thus* remains in this book. ὣς τότε (12.29), ὥς τοι (12.38) and the simile-correlative ὣς οἵ γʼ (12.255) were already rendered without it and are untouched.

---

**12.28** ὣς ἔφαθʼ, ἡμῖν δʼ αὖτʼ ἐπεπείθετο θυμὸς ἀγήνωρ.
- OLD: Thus did she speak, and our proud hearts consented and yielded within us.
- NEW: So she spoke, and our proud hearts consented and yielded within us.
