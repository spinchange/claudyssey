# Book 16 "Then"-opening revision — 39 examined, 3 kept, 36 rewritten

Warrant rule: a line keeps its opening "Then" only where the Greek carries a real
temporal adverb (τότε, δὴ τότε, καὶ τότε, ἔπειτα, αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα, temporal ἔνθα,
ἦμος-clauses). Light connectives — bare δέ, αὐτάρ, ὣς + verb, καί, γάρ, τε,
τοιγάρ, asyndeton — lose it. Every replacement still scans as six falling beats;
replacement openings are spread so no one connective becomes the new metronome
(And ×6, In turn ×4, Answering ×4, subject/verb/object-first ×10, the rest ×1–2).
Where the Greek repeats a formula verbatim, the replacement repeats too
(16.60 = 16.135 = 16.464; 16.30 = 16.68; 16.112 = 16.146).
