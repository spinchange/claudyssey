# Book 01 de-filler — all 17 filler-final lines reworked

Rules applied, as in the Book 21 pilot:

1. Every replacement is a six-beat falling line (dactyls predominating, final foot
   shortened), same as the project norm.
2. No line ends in *there*, *now*, or *then*; Book 01 turned out to have no
   warranted keeper (every filler final was the metrical enclitic νυ, a particle,
   or nothing at all in the Greek).
3. The preferred fix was reordering onto a naturally trochaic word — names
   (Calypso, Poseidon, Athena), participles (swooping, inquiring, singing),
   unstressed-final plurals (accusers, riches).
4. Masculine endings used: none (budget 3, spent 0).
5. Fidelity recovered along the way: νύμφη at 14, σε at 64, λεχέεσσι ("bed")
   kept at 366 where the draft had lost it.
6. Voice rule checked: no lexical contractions in any divine speech (32, 46, 59,
   64, 91, 188, 262, 318 are Zeus or Athena speaking).

---

**01.14**  νύμφη πότνιʼ ἔρυκε Καλυψὼ δῖα θεάων
- OLD: him did Calypso the queenly, most radiant goddess, detain there,
- NEW: him did the queenly nymph detain, bright goddess Calypso,
- νύμφη, dropped by the old line, is restored; "bright goddess" for δῖα θεάων
  (Murray's own word). The name now lands the feminine close.

**01.25**  ἀντιόων ταύρων τε καὶ ἀρνειῶν ἑκατόμβης.
- OLD: Meeting Poseidon, they offered a hundred of bulls and of rams there.
- NEW: Offering a hundred of bulls and of rams, they met Poseidon.
- Same elements resequenced onto the name.

**01.27**  Ζηνὸς ἐνὶ μεγάροισιν Ὀλυμπίου ἁθρόοι ἦσαν.
- OLD: gathered together inside of the halls of Olympian Zeus then.
- NEW: all in the halls of Olympian Zeus were gathered together.
- "all … gathered together" carries ἁθρόοι ἦσαν; "the rest of the deathless"
  (26) runs straight into "all in the halls."

**01.32**  ὢ πόποι, οἷον δή νυ θεοὺς βροτοὶ αἰτιόωνται·
- OLD: “How astonishing, mortals are always accusing the gods now!
- NEW: “How astonishing! Mortals are always the gods’ accusers!
- "the gods" kept rather than "immortals": line 31 already closes on
  "immortals," and the gods/gods echo into 33 is the Greek's own θεούς/θεῶν.
  νυ is the emphatic enclitic, not a temporal "now."

**01.46**  καὶ λίην κεῖνός γε ἐοικότι κεῖται ὀλέθρῳ·
- OLD: truly that man has been laid in the ruin that he had deserved there.
- NEW: truly that man has been laid in a ruin entirely fitting.
- ἐοικότι = "fitting," exactly; "entirely" carries the καὶ λίην … γε intensives.

**01.59**  ἧς γαίης, θανέειν ἱμείρεται. οὐδέ νυ σοί περ
- OLD: longing to die is upon him. Yet even your heart is not moved now,
- NEW: longing to die is upon him. Yet nothing has stirred your compassion,
- νυ again the emphatic enclitic, not retained; "compassion" renders the
  compunction sense of ἐντρέπεται (60 "god of Olympus!" still lands the address).

**01.63**  τὴν δʼ ἀπαμειβόμενος προσέφη νεφεληγερέτα Ζεύς·
- OLD: Zeus who assembles the cloud banks answered Athena's complaint then:
- NEW: Zeus who assembles the cloud banks made answer, addressing Athena:
- "complaint" (no warrant in the Greek) is dropped; ἀπαμειβόμενος προσέφη is
  fully rendered and the line closes on the name.

**01.64**  τέκνον ἐμόν, ποῖόν σε ἔπος φύγεν ἕρκος ὀδόντων.
- OLD: “Child of my being, what word has escaped from the fence of your teeth now?
- NEW: “Child of my being, what word past the fence of your teeth has escaped you?
- Same formula as 21.168; the pilot's exact tail plus the five-syllable vocative
  overloads the line to seven beats, so the formula's words are kept but
  resequenced. σε ("escaped *you*"), which the old line dropped, is restored.

**01.91**  πᾶσι μνηστήρεσσιν ἀπειπέμεν, οἵ τέ οἱ αἰεὶ
- OLD: there to renounce all the suitors, the men who are day after day now
- NEW: there to renounce all the suitors, the men who go on forever
- αἰεί = "forever"; "go on forever / slaughtering" (92 unchanged) keeps the
  enjambment the Greek has with 92's participial clause.

**01.102**  βῆ δὲ κατʼ Οὐλύμποιο καρήνων ἀίξασα,
- OLD: down from the crests of Olympus she swept in a flashing descent then,
- NEW: down from the crests of Olympus, in flashing descent, she went swooping,
- ἀίξασα = "swooping"; the subject "she" is load-bearing (the sentence opened at
  99 with a participle) and stays.

**01.188**  ἐξ ἀρχῆς, εἴ πέρ τε γέροντʼ εἴρηαι ἐπελθὼν
- OLD: right from the first: you may ask the old man if you go to inquire there,
- NEW: right from the first: you may ask the old man if you go inquiring,

**01.262**  ἰοὺς χρίεσθαι χαλκήρεας· ἀλλʼ ὁ μὲν οὔ οἱ
- OLD: bronze-headed arrows. But Ilus refused him the poison he sought there,
- NEW: bronze-headed arrows. But Ilus refused him the poison he wanted,

**01.318**  καὶ μάλα καλὸν ἑλών· σοὶ δʼ ἄξιον ἔσται ἀμοιβῆς.
- OLD: choosing a beautiful gift, and its worthy exchange shall be yours then.”
- NEW: choosing a beautiful gift, and yours shall be worthy requital.”
- Word for word: σοί "yours," ἔσται "shall be," ἄξιον "worthy," ἀμοιβῆς
  "requital." Nothing added.

**01.340**  οἶνον πινόντων· ταύτης δʼ ἀποπαύεʼ ἀοιδῆς
- OLD: drink of their wine; but cease from performing this sorrowful song now,
- NEW: drink of their wine; but leave off performing this sorrowful singing,
- The sing/singing ring against 339's "Sing them another" is the point of
  Penelope's plea: sing that, not this.

**01.366**  πάντες δʼ ἠρήσαντο παραὶ λεχέεσσι κλιθῆναι.
- OLD: every man praying to stretch himself out by the lady in bed there.
- NEW: every man praying to lie in the bed at the side of the lady.
- κλιθῆναι = "lie"; λεχέεσσι ("bed") kept — five dactyls and a trochee.

**01.399**  τὸν δʼ αὖτʼ Εὐρύμαχος Πολύβου πάϊς ἀντίον ηὔδα·
- OLD: Eurymachus, son of Polybus, answered the prince in return then:
- NEW: Eurymachus, son of Polybus, answered the prince directly:
- "directly" here is not a stock tail: it renders ἀντίον ("to his face").

**01.430**  τήν ποτε Λαέρτης πρίατο κτεάτεσσιν ἑοῖσιν
- OLD: whom long ago lord Laertes had bought with the wealth he possessed then,
- NEW: whom long ago Laertes had bought with his very own riches,
- "lord" (an added honorific) dropped; "very own" is the emphatic ἑοῖσιν; the
  unstressed-final plural "riches" supplies the close.

## Tally

- 17 filler-final lines reworked; 0 now end in unmotivated *there/now/then*,
  and none needed a warranted keeper (no αὐτόθι/ἔνθα/τότε at a line end in
  this book's set).
- 0 masculine endings spent (budget 3).
- Fidelity recovered: νύμφη at 14, σε at 64, "bed" (λεχέεσσι) held at 366,
  "fitting" (ἐοικότι) at 46, ἀντίον at 399; unwarranted additions removed:
  "complaint" at 63, "lord" at 430.
- Honest residue: "very" at 430 (intensive for ἑοῖσιν), "go on" at 91 (the
  iterative force of αἰεί), "in flashing descent" at 102 (inherited from the
  old line's rendering of ἀίξασα, kept alongside "swooping").
- Formula note: 64 shares its vocabulary with the pilot's 21.168 ("the fence of
  your teeth … has escaped you") but not its exact order — the longer vocative
  τέκνον ἐμόν ("Child of my being") cannot carry the pilot's tail in six beats.
  If cross-book verbatim formulas become a hard rule, revisit both together.
