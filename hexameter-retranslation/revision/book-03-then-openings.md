# Book 3 "Then"-opening pass — 36 examined, 35 rewritten, 1 kept

Warrant rule: a verse keeps its opening *Then* only where the Greek carries a real
temporal adverb doing temporal work — τότε, δὴ τότε, καὶ τότε, ἔπειτα (when it is
sequential, not formulaic), αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα, temporal ἔνθα, ἦμος-clauses. Where the
Greek has only a light connective — bare δέ, αὐτάρ, ἀτάρ, ἀλλ᾽ ὅτε, ὣς + verb,
ἦ ῥα, καί, γάρ, τε, or asyndeton — the *Then* is habitual and goes.

Two standing project rulings applied here:

1. **The reply formulas are formulas, not clocks.** τὸν δ᾽ ἠμείβετ᾽ ἔπειτα and
   τὸν/τὴν δ᾽ αὖ … ἀντίον ηὔδα open a speech; the ἔπειτα riding inside the first
   is part of the formula's shape, not a statement about time. Both become
   answer-formulas without *Then*, consistent with Books 1 and 4.
2. **αὐτὰρ ἐπεί is "But when," not "Then when."** It is the poem's standard
   pivot-to-next-scene connective, and Book 3 leans on it heavily (67, 342, 395,
   447, 461, 466, 473) — precisely the repetition that made the tic audible.

Replacement spread across the 35 rewrites (no opening exceeds ~1/5):
*But when* 6 · *In turn replied* 5 · *Answering him spoke* 3 · *Once more* 3 ·
*And when* 2 · *After* 2 · subject-first 5 · verb-first / preposition-first 6 ·
*Yet* 2 · *Soon as* 1.

## Kept as warranted

**03.453** — οἱ μὲν ἔπειτ᾽ ἀνελόντες ἀπὸ χθονὸς εὐρυοδείης
> Then from the earth with its wide roads lifting the heifer, they held her;

The book's one surviving *Then*, and the one place that earns it: ἔπειτα here is
sequential narrative, not formula — it marks the next physical step of the
sacrifice (the throat-cutting follows in 454). No crowding: its neighbors at 447,
455 and 461 all open otherwise.

## Tally

- 36 "Then"-openings examined; 35 rewritten, 1 kept warranted; residual = 1.
- No replacement opening exceeds ~1/5 of the rewrites; 11 of 35 are subject-first
  or verb/preposition-first, the most invisible options in English.
- 3 formula pairs now echo verbatim where the Greek does (67 = 473, 342 = 395,
  417 = 474).
- Incidental fidelity gains: τῆς restored at 455, ἡγεμόνευε at 386, ἐς Φηράς
  fronted at 488; filler-final *there* cleared at 455.
- Verse count unchanged at 497. No edited line ends in filler *there/now/then*.
