## Retained line-initial *Thus* (the Greek genuinely means “in this way”)

**4.15** `ὣς οἱ μὲν δαίνυντο καθʼ ὑψερεφὲς μέγα δῶμα`
- Thus through the great high-roofed halls they feasted together,
- ὣς οἱ μὲν — resumptive, “so they, for their part, were feasting …”.

**4.620** `ὣς οἱ μὲν τοιαῦτα πρὸς ἀλλήλους ἀγόρευον,`
- Thus they exchanged such words in the presence of one another.
- ὣς οἱ μὲν — the scene-closing resumptive.

**4.624** `ὣς οἱ μὲν περὶ δεῖπνον ἐνὶ μεγάροισι πένοντο.`
- Thus they continued to labor and ready the feast in the household.
- ὣς οἱ μὲν — the scene-closing resumptive, paired with 4.620.
