# Book 15 "Then"-opening revision — 65 examined, 14 kept, 51 rewritten

Two passes. First pass: the 51 sentence-capital "Then"-openings (3 kept, 48
rewritten). Second pass: the 14 enjambed lowercase "then"-openings the first
net missed (11 kept — nearly all are real ἔπειτα/τότε or when-clause apodoses —
3 rewritten). Totals above are the union.

Warrant rule: a verse may open with "Then" only where the Greek carries a real
temporal adverb (τότε, δὴ τότε, καὶ τότε, ἔπειτα, αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα, temporal ἔνθα,
ἦμος-clauses). Light connectives — bare δέ, αὐτάρ, καί, τε, ὣς + verb, ἦ ῥα,
asyndeton — and the stock reply-formulas (τὸν δ᾿ ἠμείβετ᾿, τὸν δ᾿ αὖ/αὖτε
προσέειπε, τὸν δ᾿ ἀπαμειβόμενος) do not warrant it. Every rewrite is a six-beat
falling line; no replacement opening exceeds a fifth of the rewrites ("And"
openings: 8 of 48).

## Second pass — enjambed lowercase "then"-openings (14 examined, 3 rewritten)

**15.181** τῷ κέν τοι καὶ κεῖθι θεῷ ὣς εὐχετοῴμην (τῷ κεν — inferential apodosis of the wish in 180, not temporal; Greek verbatim = 8.467)
- OLD: then even there I should pray to you just as though to a goddess.”
- NEW: so shall I pray to you even there as I pray to a goddess.”
- Formula rule: now matches revised 8.467 word for word, as the Greek does.
  Same call as 05.311 and 08.467: τῷ κε is consequence, not sequence. The
  "So now… / so shall…" pair mirrors the Greek's own οὕτω… τῷ.

**15.291** ἕλκον δʼ ἱστία λευκὰ ἐϋστρέπτοισι βοεῦσι (bare δέ in a paratactic action-chain; Greek verbatim = 2.426)
- OLD: then they hauled up the white sail with well-twisted thongs made of oxhide.
- NEW: up by the well-twisted ox-hide ropes they hauled the white sail.
- Formula rule: now matches revised 2.426 word for word, as the Greek does.

**15.537** τῷ κε τάχα γνοίης φιλότητά τε πολλά τε δῶρα (τῷ κε — apodosis of the wish in 536, not temporal)
- OLD: then very soon you would know both my friendship and many fine presents
- NEW: soon in that case you would know both my friendship and many fine presents
- τάχα "soon" supplies the opening beat; "in that case" renders τῷ κε as
  consequence (precedent: 05.311).
