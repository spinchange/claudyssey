# Book 04 de-filler — all 32 filler-final lines reworked

Rules applied (per the Book 21 pilot):

1. Every replacement is a six-beat falling line (dactyls predominating, spondaic
   substitution toward pauses, final foot preferably a falling disyllable).
2. No line ends in *there*, *now*, or *then*; no warranted keeper was needed in
   this book (04.302's αὐτόθι is absorbed by the line-opening "There," and
   04.432's τότε by the line-opening "then").
3. Preferred fix throughout: reorder so a naturally trochaic word lands last —
   names (Argos, Atreus), compounds (ox-chine, wheelwork, homeland, seaward,
   tear-drop, spearmen, herdsman), participles (smiling, plotted, resting-class
   closes), plain trochees (household, chamber, lion, leopard).
4. Masculine endings used: none (budget of 3 untouched).
5. Formulas: ἔπος τʼ ἔφατʼ ἔκ τʼ ὀνόμαζεν at 04.311 and 04.610 now closes both
   lines verbatim as "he spoke a word, and named him:". The νέον γεγαῶτʼ ἐνὶ
   οἴκῳ half-line at 04.112 now echoes 04.144's existing "left as a newborn
   child in the household." The repeated Greek line at 04.21 = 04.303
   (Τηλέμαχός θʼ ἥρως καὶ Νέστορος ἀγλαὸς υἱός) cannot be verbatim in English —
   04.21 must also carry "horses beside them" from the preceding clause — but
   04.21 now shares 04.303's "glorious offspring" vocabulary.

Adjacent lines were never edited; every join was checked in the file.

**04.21**  Τηλέμαχός θʼ ἥρως καὶ Νέστορος ἀγλαὸς υἱός,
- OLD: horses beside them— Telemachus and Nestor’s glorious son there.
- NEW: horses beside them— Telemachus and Nestor’s glorious offspring.
- The drafter's line ("the hero Telemachus, glorious son of Nestor") collapsed
  the two travelers into one person; the Greek names Telemachus AND
  Peisistratus. Rewritten to keep both, closing on "offspring" as 04.303 does.
  ἥρως goes unrendered here, as it did in the old line (04.303 gives "young").

**04.65**  ὣς φάτο, καί σφιν νῶτα βοὸς παρὰ πίονα θῆκεν
- OLD: Thus he spoke, and he lifted the fat-rich chine of the roasted ox there,
- NEW: Thus he spoke, and he lifted the fat-rich, roasted ox-chine,
- The draft's "chine of the ox that was roasted" ran to eighteen syllables and
  seven beats; compounded to "ox-chine" (the pilot's sheep-flocks/line-rule
  pattern) to restore six. "roasted" carries ὄπτʼ from the start of 4.66.

**04.112**  Τηλέμαχός θʼ, ὃν ἔλειπε νέον γεγαῶτʼ ἐνὶ οἴκῳ.
- OLD: Telemachus, whom as a newborn child he abandoned at home there.”
- NEW: Telemachus, whom as a newborn child he left in the household.”
- Formula echo of 04.144, "left as a newborn child in the household."

**04.131**  χρυσέην τʼ ἠλακάτην τάλαρόν θʼ ὑπόκυκλον ὄπασσεν
- OLD: giving a distaff of gold and a basket provided with wheels there,
- NEW: giving a distaff of gold, and a basket that ran on wheelwork,
- ὑπόκυκλον, "running on wheels beneath," closes on the compound.

**04.167**  εἴσʼ οἵ κεν κατὰ δῆμον ἀλάλκοιεν κακότητα.
- OLD: live in the country to shield him and beat his afflictions away there.”
- NEW: live in the country to shield him and drive away his affliction.”
- κακότητα is singular.

**04.174**  καί κέ οἱ Ἄργεϊ νάσσα πόλιν καὶ δώματʼ ἔτευξα,
- OLD: I would have founded a city in Argos for him, made a house there,
- NEW: I would have built him a house, and founded a city in Argos,
- The draft's "founded him a city in Argos, and built a household" left a
  four-syllable slack run after "founded"; the two clauses are swapped so the
  proper name Argos takes the trochaic close. Both acts of the Greek are kept.

**04.198**  κείρασθαί τε κόμην βαλέειν τʼ ἀπὸ δάκρυ παρειῶν.
- OLD: cutting a lock of the hair and letting a tear down the cheek there.
- NEW: cutting a lock of the hair, from the cheeks letting fall a tear-drop.
- The draft's word order scanned to seven-plus beats; the inversion restores
  six and keeps παρειῶν as a true plural ("cheeks"). δάκρυ as "tear-drop."

**04.211**  υἱέας αὖ πινυτούς τε καὶ ἔγχεσιν εἶναι ἀρίστους.
- OLD: while all his sons are intelligent men and unrivaled with spears there.
- NEW: while all his sons in their turn are wise, and unrivaled spearmen.
- αὖ restored as "in their turn"; ἔγχεσιν ἀρίστους compressed to "unrivaled
  spearmen." Draft's trailing comma corrected to a period (4.212 opens a new
  sentence).

**04.249**  τῷ ἴκελος κατέδυ Τρώων πόλιν, οἱ δʼ ἀβάκησαν
- OLD: So he went into the city of Troy, and they all were deceived there;
- NEW: So he went into the city of Troy, and they all were baffled;
- The draft's "So, in that likeness, he entered..." overloaded the line; τῷ
  ἴκελος is already carried by "So" after 4.248's "taking the shape of a
  beggar," so the old opening stands and only ἀβάκησαν ("were at a loss") is
  re-rendered, as "baffled."

**04.253**  ἀμφὶ δὲ εἵματα ἕσσα καὶ ὤμοσα καρτερὸν ὅρκον
- OLD: put the clothes on his body, and sworn a powerful oath there,
- NEW: put the clothes on his body, and sworn him an oath of great power,
- Draft's "swore" broke the participial chain governed by 4.252's "I had
  bathed... and rubbed"; restored to "sworn." "him" (the oath's recipient,
  Odysseus) is the one added word.

**04.302**  οἱ μὲν ἄρʼ ἐν προδόμῳ δόμου αὐτόθι κοιμήσαντο,
- OLD: There in the forecourt both of the traveling heroes reposed then,
- NEW: There in the palace forecourt the two lay down and slumbered,
- αὐτόθι is carried by the line-opening "There"; δόμου by "palace." The old
  line's "traveling" was itself unwarranted and is not kept; 4.303 supplies
  the pair's names.

**04.311**  Τηλεμάχῳ δὲ παρῖζεν, ἔπος τʼ ἔφατʼ ἔκ τʼ ὀνόμαζεν·
- OLD: Close by Telemachus then he sat and addressed him by name there:
- NEW: Close by Telemachus sitting, he spoke a word, and named him:
- Formula tail verbatim with 04.610. Draft's "Close beside" made seven feet;
  "Close by" restores six.

**04.318**  ἐσθίεταί μοι οἶκος, ὄλωλε δὲ πίονα ἔργα,
- OLD: Men consume my household, and all my abundant fields are destroyed now;
- NEW: Men are devouring my household, and my fertile farmlands have perished;
- ἐσθίεται as true progressive; ὄλωλε as "have perished"; πίονα ἔργα as
  "fertile farmlands." The draft's "ruin has fallen on my rich farmlands" ran
  a beat over.

**04.344**  κὰδ δʼ ἔβαλε κρατερῶς, κεχάροντο δὲ πάντες Ἀχαιοί,
- OLD: hurled him down with tremendous force, while Achaeans rejoiced there—
- NEW: hurled him mightily down, to the joy of all the Achaeans—
- κρατερῶς as the adverb "mightily" (the old "with tremendous force" was
  padding a paraphrase); πάντες kept; closes on the name.

**04.410**  πάντα δέ τοι ἐρέω ὀλοφώια τοῖο γέροντος.
- OLD: I shall reveal every cunning device the old god may employ there.
- NEW: I shall reveal to you all of the tricks of the old man’s cunning.
- τοι = "to you"; τοῖο γέροντος = "of the old man" (the old line's "god" was
  an error — Proteus is "the ancient" throughout this episode). Eidothea
  speaks: no contractions.

**04.432**  καὶ τότε δὴ παρὰ θῖνα θαλάσσης εὐρυπόροιο
- OLD: then I proceeded along the shore of the wide-wayed sea there,
- NEW: then, along by the shore of the wide-wayed sea, I proceeded,
- Line-opening "then" is warranted by καὶ τότε δή; the verse continues 4.431's
  sentence, so the draft's capital "Then" is corrected to lowercase.

**04.456**  ἀλλʼ ἦ τοι πρώτιστα λέων γένετʼ ἠυγένειος,
- OLD: First he became a tremendous lion with beautiful mane there,
- NEW: Truly he first became a goodly-bearded lion,
- The old "tremendous" had no Greek behind it and the draft kept it as
  "enormous"; dropped. ἦ τοι = "truly"; ἠυγένειος rendered literally,
  "goodly-bearded." A deliberately spondaic-trochaic line before the
  transformation catalogue.

**04.457**  αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα δράκων καὶ πάρδαλις ἠδὲ μέγας σῦς·
- OLD: then a tremendous serpent, a leopard, a mountain-sized boar there;
- NEW: then in turn a serpent, a mighty boar, and a leopard;
- μέγας belongs to the boar, not the serpent (the old line misplaced it, the
  draft kept the misplacement and diluted μέγας to "of the mountains"). Boar
  and leopard swap places so the catalogue closes on the trochee; αὐτὰρ
  ἔπειτα = "then in turn."

**04.520**  ἂψ δὲ θεοὶ οὖρον στρέψαν, καὶ οἴκαδʼ ἵκοντο,
- OLD: gods turned the wind to an excellent course, and at home they arrived then.
- NEW: gods turned the wind to a favorable course, and they came to their homeland.

**04.536**  οὐδέ τις Ἀτρεΐδεω ἑτάρων λίπεθʼ οἵ οἱ ἕποντο,
- OLD: Not one companion of Atreus’ son who had followed remained there,
- NEW: Not one was left of the comrades that followed the son of Atreus,
- The draft's "of Atreus' son, of those who had followed" ran seven beats and
  kept the old line's dangling relative. Recast so 4.536–537 parallel: "of
  Atreus, / nor one man of Aegisthus." Dactylic name close (Atreus), like
  "hecatombs" at 04.582.

**04.582**  στῆσα νέας, καὶ ἔρεξα τεληέσσας ἑκατόμβας.
- OLD: up the heaven-descended river, and offered full hecatombs there.
- NEW: up the heaven-descended river, and offered full hecatombs.
- Draft's "complete hecatombs" put an iamb in the fifth foot; the old line's
  own "full" (τεληέσσας) restores the scan. Dactylic close accepted.

**04.609**  ὣς φάτο, μείδησεν δὲ βοὴν ἀγαθὸς Μενέλαος,
- OLD: Thus he spoke, and Menelaus renowned for the war cry smiled there;
- NEW: Thus he spoke, and Menelaus, renowned for the war cry, was smiling;

**04.610**  χειρί τέ μιν κατέρεξεν ἔπος τʼ ἔφατʼ ἔκ τʼ ὀνόμαζεν·
- OLD: stroking him there with his hand, he addressed him aloud by his name there:
- NEW: stroking him gently with his hand, he spoke a word, and named him:
- Formula tail verbatim with 04.311. "gently" unpacks the caress inside
  κατέρεξεν and is the logged added word. Draft's capital "Stroking" corrected
  to lowercase — the line continues 4.609's sentence.

**04.640**  ἀγρῶν ἢ μήλοισι παρέμμεναι ἠὲ συβώτῃ.
- OLD: close in the fields with the flocks or beside the keeper of swine there.
- NEW: close in the fields with the flocks, or out with the swine and their herdsman.
- συβώτῃ unfolded as "the swine and their herdsman" for the trochaic close.

**04.692**  ἄλλον κʼ ἐχθαίρῃσι βροτῶν, ἄλλον κε φιλοίη.
- OLD: kings: they may hate one mortal and love one mortal in turn there.
- NEW: kings: they may hate one man among mortals, and cherish another.
- The draft appended a closing quotation mark, but Penelope's speech runs on
  through 4.693; removed.

**04.766**  μνηστῆρας δʼ ἀπάλαλκε κακῶς ὑπερηνορέοντας.
- OLD: beat the oppressive and wickedly arrogant suitors away now.”
- NEW: drive far away the oppressive and wickedly arrogant suitors.”

**04.771**  ἀρτύει, οὐδέ τι οἶδεν ὅ οἱ φόνος υἷι τέτυκται.
- OLD: here with one of us, knowing no part of the death of her son there.”
- NEW: here with one of us suitors, not knowing her son’s death is plotted.”
- The draft's "with no knowledge that..." left a five-syllable slack run.
  "suitors" is the logged added word — it names the ἄμμι of 4.770 explicitly
  (the speakers are the suitors themselves) and restores the third beat.

**04.784**  τεύχεα δέ σφʼ ἤνεικαν ὑπέρθυμοι θεράποντες.
- OLD: High-hearted servants carried the weapons and placed them aboard there.
- NEW: High-hearted servants carried the weapons and stowed them aboard her.
- "her" is the ship, matching 4.785's existing "they moored her." Draft's
  trailing comma corrected to a period (4.785 opens a new sentence).

**04.802**  ἐς θάλαμον δʼ εἰσῆλθε παρὰ κληῖδος ἱμάντα,
- OLD: Into the chamber it came by the strap that secured the door bolt there,
- NEW: By the strap of the door-bolt it entered into the chamber,
- The draft's "the strap of the door bolt's fastening" tripled the hardware
  (strap, bolt, fastening) for one Greek phrase; reordered instead so
  "chamber" closes the line — παρὰ κληῖδος ἱμάντα, ἐς θάλαμον εἰσῆλθε, exact.

**04.821**  ἢ ὅ γε τῶν ἐνὶ δήμῳ, ἵνʼ οἴχεται, ἢ ἐνὶ πόντῳ·
- OLD: whether among the men of the land where he went or at sea there.
- NEW: whether among the men of the land where he journeyed, or seaward,
- "seaward" for ἢ ἐνὶ πόντῳ, in the poem's -ward idiom. Draft's "the land he
  has journeyed to" smoothed to "where he journeyed."

**04.833**  ἤ που ἔτι ζώει καὶ ὁρᾷ φάος ἠελίοιο,
- OLD: whether he still lives somewhere and looks on the light of the sun there,
- NEW: whether he still is alive somewhere, and looks on the sunlight,

**04.840**  κούρη Ἰκαρίοιο· φίλον δέ οἱ ἦτορ ἰάνθη,
- OLD: started awake from her sleep, and the heart in her bosom was warmed then,
- NEW: started awake from her sleep, and the heart in her bosom grew warmer,
- The draft's "the heart deep within her bosom" ran to nineteen syllables;
  "deep within" (no Greek warrant) dropped.
