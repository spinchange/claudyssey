**05.28** ἦ ῥα καὶ Ἑρμείαν, υἱὸν φίλον, ἀντίον ηὔδα
- OLD: Thus having spoken, he turned to the well-loved son Hermes and told him:
- NEW: He spoke, and he turned to the well-loved son Hermes and told him:
- ἦ ῥα is not ὣς φάτο and is not participial; the old line flattened it into the
  ὣς εἰπών rendering. "He spoke, and" keeps ἦ ῥα καί distinct from both fixed
  formulas without inventing a third stock phrase.

**05.43** ὣς ἔφατʼ, οὐδʼ ἀπίθησε διάκτορος ἀργεϊφόντης.
- OLD: Thus did he speak, and the guide who had slaughtered Argus obeyed him.
- NEW: So he spoke, and the guide who had slaughtered Argus obeyed him.

**05.116** ὣς φάτο, ῥίγησεν δὲ Καλυψώ, δῖα θεάων,
- OLD: Thus did he speak, and the radiant goddess Calypso was trembling.
- NEW: So he spoke, and the radiant goddess Calypso was trembling.

**05.171** ὣς φάτο, ῥίγησεν δὲ πολύτλας δῖος Ὀδυσσεύς,
- OLD: Thus did she speak, and the steadfast and noble Odysseus trembled.
- NEW: So she spoke, and the steadfast and noble Odysseus trembled.
- Calypso is the speaker; subject follows the Greek.

**05.180** ὣς φάτο, μείδησεν δὲ Καλυψὼ δῖα θεάων,
- OLD: Thus did he speak, and the radiant goddess Calypso was smiling.
- NEW: So he spoke, and the radiant goddess Calypso was smiling.
- 116/171/180 share one Greek shape (ὣς φάτο, + aorist + subject); the English
  now repeats with it, differing only where the Greek differs.

**05.225** ὣς ἔφατʼ, ἠέλιος δʼ ἄρʼ ἔδυ καὶ ἐπὶ κνέφας ἦλθεν·
- OLD: Thus he spoke, while the sun sank down and the twilight descended.
- NEW: So he spoke, while the sun sank down and the twilight descended.

**05.451** ὣς φάθʼ, ὁ δʼ αὐτίκα παῦσεν ἑὸν ῥόον, ἔσχε δὲ κῦμα,
- OLD: Thus did he speak, and the god stayed the current and held back the billow,

## Not changed, with warrant

**05.68** ἡ δʼ αὐτοῦ τετάνυστο περὶ σπείους γλαφυροῖο
- KEPT: Over the hollow cave a flourishing vine had been trained there,
- αὐτοῦ ("right in that place") is in the Greek; the deictic close is earned,
  exactly as at 21.90.

**05.313** ὣς ἄρα μιν εἰπόντʼ ἔλασεν μέγα κῦμα κατʼ ἄκρης
- KEPT: Just as he spoke, an enormous wave came rushing from high up,
- Accusative participle in a temporal clause, not the finite ὣς φάτο formula and
  not the ὣς εἰπών nominative participial. Distinct Greek, distinct English.

**05.430** καὶ τὸ μὲν ὣς ὑπάλυξε, παλιρρόθιον δέ μιν αὖτις
- KEPT: Thus he escaped it; but backward the water came leaping against him,
- ὣς here genuinely means "in this way" (τὸ μὲν ὣς ὑπάλυξε = "that much he thus
  escaped"). "Thus" earns its place. **Counted as residual_thus.**

**05.474** ὣς ἄρα οἱ φρονέοντι δοάσσατο κέρδιον εἶναι·
- KEPT: Yet as he weighed it, this seemed in his mind to be greatly the better.
- Not a speech formula; no "Thus" opening. See note in the editor's summary about
  its twin at 6.145.
