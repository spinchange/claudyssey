# Book 21 "Then"-opening pass — 43 examined, 9 kept, 34 rewritten

Warrant rule applied: a line-initial "Then" is WARRANTED only where the Greek line
carries a real temporal adverb or apodosis structure — τότε, δὴ τότε, καὶ τότε,
ἔπειτα, αὐτὰρ ἔπειτα, temporal ἔνθα, or the apodosis of an ἦμος/ἐπεί/ὅτε/εἰ-clause
("But when… then…", "if… then…"). A "Then" sitting on a light connective — bare δέ,
αὐτάρ, ἀτάρ, ὣς + verb, ἦ ῥα, καί, γάρ, τε, ἄρα, or asyndeton — is HABITUAL and
was rewritten.

Result: every one of the 30 capitalized "Then"-openings proved habitual (none of
their Greek lines has τότε or ἔπειτα); four lowercase line-initial "then"s were
habitual as well (119, 139, 166, 432). The nine survivors are all lowercase
clause-apodosis "then"s bound to a "But when…"/"if…" protasis. No three of them
fall within a ten-line window, so no crowding variation was needed.

Formula echoes restored where the Greek repeats verbatim (pilot rule 5):
84 = 167 = 287 (Ἀντίνοος δʼ ἐνένιπεν…) now share 167's wording; 139 = 166
(ἂψ δʼ αὖτις κατʼ ἄρʼ ἕζετʼ…) now identical; 311 = 330 (τὸν δʼ αὖτε προσέειπε
περίφρων Πηνελόπεια) now identical.

Opening-variety tally across 34 rewrites: prepositional-phrase-first 8, plain
subject-first 8, "And/and" 5 (two of them the 139/166 formula pair), "But" 3,
object/pronoun-first ("Him/To him/Water…") 4, participle-first 3, adverb-first
("Once again", "At dawn", "Up") 3 — no opening word exceeds a fifth of the set.
"At once" (67) sits on αὐτίκα in the Greek; "Once again" (130) on αὖτις;
"in turn" (256, 401) on αὖτε/αὖ.
