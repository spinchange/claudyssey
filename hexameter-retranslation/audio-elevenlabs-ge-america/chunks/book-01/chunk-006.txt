“Stranger, because you inquire of me closely and ask me these questions,
once this household was destined for riches and honor abundant,
while that excellent man stayed here among his own kindred and people.
Now have the gods, with a purpose of evil, decided another
fate: they made him unseen above all other men who have vanished.
Never would I have been grieving like this, had he fallen and perished
there with his own companions away in the country of Trojans,
or in the arms of his friends after winding the war to completion.
Then the Achaeans together would raise him a tomb in his honor,
and for his son he would win great glory to live in the future.
Now he has vanished without any glory, the storm winds have snatched him;
gone out of sight and unheard of, but leaving me anguish and mourning.
Nor is it only for him I am grieving and groaning in sorrow;
other calamitous troubles the gods have fashioned upon me.
All of the noblest men who are rulers among all the islands,
men of Dulichium, Same, and the forested isle of Zacynthus,
all who possess any lordship throughout craggy Ithaca also,
all of them court my own mother and ruin the wealth of my household.
She neither finally refuses the hateful marriage nor brings it
ever to pass; while they keep on consuming and wasting the household.
Soon they will shatter and tear me to pieces as well as my riches.”
