# Book 5: Marked performance text

This companion to [the clean reading text](book-05.md) marks one intended oral
delivery of the English hexameter. An acute accent marks each governing beat;
vertical bars divide the six feet. Capitalization makes each beat easy to find.
Unmarked syllables may still take secondary stress in natural speech. A shortened
foot is intentional, not a missing beat.

The marks describe **accentual English** meter. They do not assign Greek-style
quantity to English vowels.
