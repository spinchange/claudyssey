# Adjudication Report

The translation remains highly faithful across *Odyssey* 7.174-347, with four localized findings. One is a contextual number error introduced by carrying plural hospitality wording into service of the single guest. The reviewers agree on a weakened directive at 7.226-227. A consequential temporal error at 7.317-320 places Odysseus' sleep before rather than during his voyage, and note L346 overstates a close formulaic reworking as verbatim recurrence.

## Especially Successful Renderings

- **7.215-221:** The belly speech preserves personification, coercive force, bodily indignity, and the tension between hunger and grief.
- **7.241-266:** Odysseus' selective Calypso narrative retains chronology, epistemic limits, and his refusal to disclose his identity.
- **7.267-289:** The shipwreck, retreat from the rocks, river refuge, and exhausted sleep preserve a difficult sequence.

## Calibration Implications

Exact registered wording does not shield a recurrence from semantic review when its grammatical number conflicts with the new context. Apply the existing "urged" speech-act anchor consistently to verbs of bidding. Test temporal adverbs with coordinated syntax when simultaneity matters. Recurrence notes using "verbatim" require line-by-line verification.
