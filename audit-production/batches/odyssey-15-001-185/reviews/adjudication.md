## Especially Successful Renderings

- **15.27-42:** Athena's ambush warning preserves the suitors' intent, the
  geography of their watch, the night-sailing instruction, and the tactical
  route through Eumaeus.
- **15.68-85:** Menelaus' measured hospitality keeps the antitheses between
  excessive love and hate, expelling and detaining, and cherishing and
  speeding, before moving naturally into his generous tour proposal.
- **15.92-159:** The meal, descent to the treasure chamber, gift presentation,
  stowing, second meal, libation, and farewell retain their ritual order and
  agency with unusual clarity.

## Calibration Implications

Notes should not turn a directly stated and later broadly corroborated warning
into deception merely because one specific detail lacks independent repetition.
Agency expressed by a middle verb must survive when it controls the force of a
gendered accusation. As calibrated in Book 13, singular indefinite `τινα`
should not become an explicit plural because later events make plurality
likely. Numerical interval and exact-recurrence claims require direct
line-by-line verification; auditing a false claim about a Greek recurrence is
distinct from counting a fixed-English-wording deviation owned by automation.
