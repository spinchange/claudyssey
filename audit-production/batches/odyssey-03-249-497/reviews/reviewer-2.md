## Especially Successful Renderings

1. **3.269–272:** “he willing, she willing” sharply preserves `ἐθέλων ἐθέλουσαν`, including Clytemnestra’s consequential agency without flattening the preceding resistance and divine doom.
2. **3.345–355:** Nestor’s indignant hospitality speech keeps the force of `Ζεὺς τό γ’ ἀλεξήσειε`, the cloak-and-bedding contrast, and his intergenerational commitment to host Odysseus’ son.
3. **3.447–460:** The sacrificial sequence accurately preserves the distinct ritual actions and agents, including the axe-blow, women’s ritual cry, throat-cutting, thigh-pieces, fat, raw flesh, libation, and five-tined forks.
