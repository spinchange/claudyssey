| R1-002, line 408 and note L408 | Rejected | `λευκοί, ἀποστίλβοντες ἀλείφατος` does not unambiguously assert that literal oil coats the stones. The genitive characterizes their oil-like gleam or polish, and the ancient gloss explicitly supplies an omitted `ὡς`: `λείπει τὸ ὡς ... ἀντὶ τοῦ ὡς ἐλαίου`, “as oil.” Thus “gleaming as if with oil” is a defensible rendering, and the note does not displace an established concrete fact. [Eustathius, commentary on Odyssey 3.408](https://www.matia.gr/pisth/pdf/pg_migne/Eustathius_Thessalonicensis_PG_135_136/Commentarii_ad_Homeri_Odysseam_i.pdf) |
