# Adjudication Report

Reviewer 1 proposed two findings and Reviewer 2 proposed one. The proposals at
9.51-52 identify the same unsupported atmospheric detail and are merged. That
finding is sustained. Reviewer 1's note proposal at 9.166-167 is rejected:
although “the reconnaissance is aural” is an inaccurate isolated label, the
same note quotes the visible smoke, explicitly says “to see” it, and develops
smoke as a visible horizon sign. Read as a coherent passage, the note does not
materially conceal the mixed visual and auditory evidence.

## Calibration Implications

Concrete scenic details introduced while rendering a temporal adverb remain
auditable additions even when they are conventional poetic imagery and leave
the larger event intact. Conversely, adjudicate a note as a coherent passage:
an inaccurate isolated modality label should not become a firm finding when
the same note explicitly presents and correctly interprets the supposedly
excluded visual evidence.
