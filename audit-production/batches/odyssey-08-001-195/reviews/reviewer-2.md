# Reviewer 2 Report

The translation is consistently strong across *Odyssey* 8.1-195, particularly in the Demodocus sequence, Odysseus' concealed grief, and the exchange at the games. I find two material issues: note L35 assigns unsupported officer roles, and line 124 collapses a ploughing range into the width of a furrow.

| ID | Lines | Category | Severity | Confidence | Finding | Greek evidence | Suggested direction |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| R2-001 | Note L35; 35-37, 48-54 | ADDITION | MINOR | 0.84 | The note's "standard crew of fifty plus two officers" assigns two of the fifty-two selected young men officer roles not established by the poem. The same note calls all fifty-two picked oarsmen and rowers. | Alcinous orders `κούρω ... δύω καὶ πεντήκοντα / κρινάσθων`, fifty-two young men to be chosen; all are addressed together, and no two are separated as officers. | Call them fifty-two selected crewmen or rowers; omit the unsupported division. |
| R2-002 | 8.124-125 and note L124 | LEXICAL | MODERATE | 0.99 | "The width of a mule-team's furrow" reduces a ploughing range or furrow length to the narrow width of one furrow, materially collapsing Clytoneus' winning margin. | `ὅσσον τʼ ἐν νειῷ οὖρον πέλει ἡμιόνοιιν, / τόσσον ὑπεκπροθέων...`: `οὖρον` is the range or furrow distance associated with a mule team, and `τόσσον` makes that span his lead. | Use "by the length of a furrow a mule-team ploughs" or "by a mule-team's ploughing range." |

## Especially Successful Renderings

1. **8.63-70:** The Muse's deprivation and gift and the herald's accommodation of Demodocus' blindness remain precise.
2. **8.83-95:** The iterative singing, concealment, weeping, wiping, libation, and renewed mourning preserve aspect and recognition.
