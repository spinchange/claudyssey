## Calibration Implications

Notes should not convert inferred administrative convenience into Homeric fact. Concrete technical vocabulary can be minor or moderate depending on whether it misnames equipment or changes scale. Preserve the culpable force of the `ἀτασθαλίαι` family. At 8.153, directed pressure in the whole phrase is sufficient despite the less literal "urge."
