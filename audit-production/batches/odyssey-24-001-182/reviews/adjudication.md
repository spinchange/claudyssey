# Adjudication — *Odyssey* 24.1–182

## Overall Assessment

The translation is vivid and generally faithful, with especially strong handling of Hermes’ procession, Achilles’ funeral, and Amphimedon’s account of the web stratagem and slaughter. The six sustained findings comprise one moderate comparative-attachment error and five localized verse-or-note problems involving invented action, chronology, false exclusivity, funerary function, or pronoun reference.

Eleven firm reviewer IDs resolve to seven distinct proposal keys. Four true duplicate pairs were merged. Six distinct proposals are sustained and one is rejected. The Hermes-wand finding is normalized to ADDITION because the note preserves the rod’s stated capacity while inventing an exercise of that capacity on Calypso’s island.

R1-003 is rejected. A tomb deliberately built on a projecting headland to be conspicuous to present and future men at sea readily supports the note’s literary description of a coastal navigation-mark.

Adjudication status: COMPLETE. No proposal remains unresolved. No fixed-wording-only FORMULA deviation was counted.

## Calibration Implications

- Capacity does not establish prior exercise of that capacity.
- `μετά` must be tested for rank as well as temporal sequence.
- Parallel settings do not establish exact simultaneity.
- Exclusive thematic claims require checking cross-poem counterexamples.
- Completion of a delaying object does not erase its separate practical function.
- Pronouns must be checked when the nearest English antecedent reverses alignment.
