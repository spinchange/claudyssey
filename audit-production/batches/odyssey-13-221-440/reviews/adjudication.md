# Adjudication Report

Reviewer 1 proposed three findings and Reviewer 2 proposed three. Their
proposals about the note's claim that Athena repeats Anticleia's three-line
report verbatim identify the same issue and are merged. Five distinct findings
are sustained: four affect explanatory notes and one combines two repeated
verse expansions with the attached note. The notes misattribute Athena's
agricultural catalogue to Odysseus, overstate a one-line verbatim recurrence as
three lines, add an unstated rolling mechanism and Cyclopean scale, and invent
a third exact authority for a clause that has occurred only twice. The verse
twice turns singular indefinite `τινα` into explicit “more than one,” and the
second note repeats that unsupported plural. No fixed-wording-only formula
deviation is counted.

  Telemachus, and the ambush preserve agency and sequence.

## Calibration Implications

Cross-passage attribution in notes must distinguish new catalogue material
from genuine echoes. Claims that multiple lines or authorities are “verbatim”
require exact line-by-line recurrence counts; correcting such a false Greek
recurrence claim is distinct from enforcing fixed English register. Concrete
mechanisms and dimensions should not be supplied merely to strengthen a
literary parallel. Finally, Greek singular indefinites should not become
explicit plurals when the plurality adds a more specific prediction, even if
later narrative events make multiple deaths unsurprising.
