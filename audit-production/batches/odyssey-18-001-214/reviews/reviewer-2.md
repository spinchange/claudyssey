# Reviewer 2 — *Odyssey* 18.1–214

The passage is generally vivid and faithful, particularly in the Irus characterization, fight choreography, and Odysseus’ warning to Amphinomus. I found six firm issues: one moderate displacement of a central philosophical image and five localized errors. Fixed-wording-only formula deviations are excluded.

## Findings

| ID | Lines | Category | Severity | Confidence | Finding | Greek evidence | Suggested direction |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| R2-001 | 15 | LEXICAL | MINOR | 0.98 | “Man possessed” literalizes emphatic vocative `δαιμόνιε` into supernatural possession. Nothing diagnoses Irus as possessed. | `δαιμόνιʼ, οὔτε τί σε ῥέζω κακὸν` is a forceful address followed by denial of harm. | Use a forceful nonsupernatural address. |
| R2-002 | 128 note | MISTRANSLATION | MINOR | 0.99 | The note says `ἐπητής` came from “Penelope’s own report” at 13.332, but that is Athena’s direct speech. | Athena begins speaking at 13.329 and supplies the praise at 13.332. | Attribute it to Athena. |
| R2-003 | 136–137 and note L136 | LEXICAL | MODERATE | 0.89 | “It is weather” replaces the comparison between the human mind and whatever day or daily condition Zeus brings with an expressly meteorological metaphor; the note reinforces it. | `τοῖος γὰρ νόος ... οἷον ἐπʼ ἦμαρ ἄγησι` compares mind to the day Zeus brings. | Preserve the day-and-fortune comparison directly. |
| R2-004 | 175–176 | LEXICAL | MINOR | 0.94 | “The age you prayed … so long to see” changes `μάλιστα`, “especially/above all,” into duration. | `ὃν σὺ μάλιστα ἠρῶ ... γενειήσαντα ἰδέσθαι`. | Use “whom above all you prayed to see bearded.” |

| R2-006 | 187–191 note | ADDITION | MINOR | 0.86 | The note says Athena pours sleep “precisely so” she can beautify Penelope without consent.
