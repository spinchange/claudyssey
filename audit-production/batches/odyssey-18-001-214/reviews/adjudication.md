# Adjudication — *Odyssey* 18.1–214

Seven distinct findings are sustained from ten firm proposals. Three duplicate pairs were merged. The most consequential issue is the recasting of the human mind's dependence on the day Zeus brings as expressly meteorological “weather”; the remaining findings are localized lexical or explanatory-note errors. No proposal is rejected, uncertain, or unresolved.

## Rejected and Unresolved Proposals

No firm proposal was rejected or unresolved. The below-threshold alternatives were checked: a long-standing prayer does not make duration equivalent to `μάλιστα`, and Penelope's refusal makes the coercive reading plausible but not Athena's explicitly stated purpose. Other concerns about recognition, teeth, libation, the added sail image, and grief remain below threshold because coherent context preserves their operative meaning.

Severity totals: CRITICAL 0; MAJOR 0; MODERATE 1; MINOR 6. Total findings: 7. Reviewer Jaccard: 42.86% (`3/7`). Findings per 100 records: 3.27.

## Calibration Implications

Unsupported diagnostic senses in vocatives are normally lexical. Added governing metaphors are reportable when they replace a gnomic conceptual relation. Do not convert emphasis into duration merely because duration is plausible. Notes must distinguish interpretive ethical readings from explicit divine purpose and verify chronology, agency, and universal claims.
