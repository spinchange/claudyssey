Overall, the translation is semantically close and usually preserves the social pressure, irony, and epic diction of the Greek without over-literalizing. I find one firm material issue, in an explanatory note rather than the verse translation itself: the note's account of Athena's disguises appears to misstate the logic of the Noemon episode.
