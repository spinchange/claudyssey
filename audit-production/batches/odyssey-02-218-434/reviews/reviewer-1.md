Overall, the translation is semantically close and often preserves Homeric force with controlled poetic recasting. I found two firm material issues: one in Leocritus' reply, where the comparative force of "more numerous" is attached in the wrong direction, and one in an explanatory note that misstates Athena's disguise sequence.
