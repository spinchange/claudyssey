Especially successful renderings:

- **1.225–229:** The unpacking of `οὐκ ἔρανος` as “plainly no man here brought his own share” preserves both the feast taxonomy and Athena’s pointed accusation, while “burn with anger” gives `νεμεσσήσαιτο` appropriate force.
- **1.267–278:** The translation accurately preserves Athena’s uncertainty formula, her forceful commands, and the registered bride-gift refrain; “I charge you” is particularly effective for `ἄνωγα`.
- **1.374–380:** Telemachus’ ultimatum is rhetorically sharp and retains the repeated `νήποινον` logic through “nothing paid” and “nothing paid for you,” preserving the movement from uncompensated consumption to uncompensated death.
