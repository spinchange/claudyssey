# Production Audit Report: *Odyssey* 1.156-444

## Result

Two independent reviewers and one adjudicator assessed the 289 verse records
that complete Book 1 after the original pilot. Six localized findings were
sustained: one `MODERATE` mistranslation in a translator's note and five `MINOR`
departures from the authoritative formula or vocabulary policy. No `MAJOR` or
`CRITICAL` findings were found.

| Lines | Severity | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1.184 note | MODERATE | “Copper out, iron in” reverses the voyage's commodities; Mentes carries iron out and seeks copper. |
| 1.191, 362 | MINOR | “Serving-woman/women” violates the registered “handmaid/attendant women” policy. |
| 1.265-266 | MINOR | The translation varies a fixed two-line Odysseus-and-suitors refrain. |
| 1.368 | MINOR | “Insolent past all bearing” varies registered “overweening insolence.” |
| 1.420 | MINOR | “So spoke Telemachus” varies the registered “So he spoke.” |
| 1.435 | MINOR | “House-slaves” broadens feminine `δμῳάων` and violates “slave-women” policy. |

The proposals at 1.280 (rowers/oars) and 1.398 (slaves won as plunder) remain
uncertain and are excluded from the totals.

Across the completed Book 1 pilot and production batch, all 444 verse records
have been audited. Book 1 has nine adjudicated findings: four `MODERATE` and
five `MINOR`, or **2.03 findings per 100 records**, with no `MAJOR` or
`CRITICAL` findings.

## Workflow Decision

Continue with dual independent review and mandatory adjudication. The reviewers
agreed on two firm proposals but each caught formula-policy violations the
other missed. Future calibration should emphasize fixed-register enforcement,
compensation before escalating lexical differences, and Greek unmarked subject
shifts across coordinated actions.

## Integrity

The batch contains 289 exactly aligned Greek and English records and all 19
cited translation notes. Completion verification checks both original source
hashes and prepared-artifact hashes.
