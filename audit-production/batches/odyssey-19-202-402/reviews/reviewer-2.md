# Reviewer 2 — Semantic Review

The batch is semantically strong overall. I found seven countable issues: one moderate lexical weakening and six minor grammar or commentary inaccuracies. Most problems occur in the notes rather than the translation itself. No major or critical errors were found.

## Successful Passages

1. **19.203–212:** The translation preserves the tension between truth and deception and handles the snow-melting simile effectively, including Penelope’s concealed tears and Odysseus’ controlled expression.

2. **19.218–247, 249–250:** The garment, brooch, and Eurybates details function clearly as increasingly specific recognition tokens while retaining the uncertainty surrounding whether the stranger personally witnessed them.

3. **19.317–348:** The hospitality sequence and Odysseus’ selective refusal of the footwashing are rendered coherently, preserving both the social courtesy and the disguised hero’s tactical qualification.
