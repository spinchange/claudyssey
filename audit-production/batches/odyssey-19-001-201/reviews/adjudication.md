## Rejected and Unresolved Proposals

R2-001 is rejected: the imperfect `ἐφῖζε` and the named established chair support a customary sense, and “always sat” functions as ordinary habitual English rather than a consequential universal. No proposal is unresolved. Other concerns about habitual sleeping, jurisdiction, grief, “shouted me down,” Cretan verifiability, Minos' cycle, and public provision remain below threshold because coherent context prevents material error.

Severity totals: CRITICAL 0; MAJOR 0; MODERATE 0; MINOR 9. Findings: 9. Reviewer Jaccard: 60% (`6/10`). Findings per 100 records: 4.48.

## Calibration Implications

Priority words remain factual claims. A supported habitual imperfect may use idiomatic “always.” Notes may infer household significance but not invent autobiographical memory. Similes do not establish literal office-holding. Preserve adverbial constancy, grammatical recipients, and culpable force in modern lexical choices.
