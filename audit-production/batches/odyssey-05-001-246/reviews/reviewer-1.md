## Especially Successful Renderings

1. **Lines 154-155:** “he had to ... unwilling man by willing goddess” sharply preserves `ἀνάγκῃ` and the compact consent contrast `παρʼ οὐκ ἐθέλων ἐθελούσῃ`, without obscuring agency.
2. **Lines 206-224:** Calypso’s offer and Odysseus’ refusal retain the full logic of the Greek: the destined suffering, immortality, Penelope’s acknowledged inferiority, and Odysseus’ overriding desire for homecoming all remain distinct and forceful.
3. **Lines 234-246:** The carpentry sequence renders the tools, materials, actions, count, and skilled workmanship precisely while remaining clear English verse; `ξέσσε δʼ ἐπισταμένως καὶ ἐπὶ στάθμην ἴθυνεν` is especially well captured by “dressed them smooth with skill, and trued them to the line.”
