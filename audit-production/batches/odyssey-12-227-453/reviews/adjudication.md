# Adjudication Report

Reviewer 1 proposed three findings and Reviewer 2 proposed five. The proposals
about the note's universal eyewitness claim and the verse's six-day duration
are duplicate pairs and are merged. Six distinct findings are sustained: two
affect the translated verse and four affect explanatory notes. The verse
findings change the companions' sleepiness into sleeplessness and six days of
continuous feasting into a meal six days later. The notes add an unsupported
sight-before-sound sequence and sleep count, overstate a partial recurrence as
wholly verbatim, and falsely make the rest of Odysseus' account uniformly
eyewitness testimony. No fixed-wording-only formula deviation is counted.

## Calibration Implications

Claims that a passage is “verbatim” remain auditable when the note makes a
false claim about the Greek recurrence; that is distinct from a
fixed-English-wording deviation owned by automation. Notes should not infer
sight-versus-sound order from arrival and hearing language that states only
location. Numbered thematic patterns require every member to fit the stated
comparison class. Universal claims about narrator knowledge should be tested
against nearby explicitly unwitnessed events. In verse, distinguish a need for
sleep from sleeplessness, and preserve accusatives of temporal extent when the
duration controls the narrative sequence.
