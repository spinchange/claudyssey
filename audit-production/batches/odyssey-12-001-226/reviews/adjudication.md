# Adjudication Report

Reviewer 1 proposed seven findings and Reviewer 2 proposed five. The proposals
about Scylla's measured necks, the disclosure-to-withholding interval, the
“within the week” chronology, and the rowlocks are duplicate pairs and are
merged, yielding eight distinct proposal keys. Seven findings are retained.
The funeral-sequence proposal is modified from MODERATE to MINOR because
`θάπτομεν` can introduce funeral rites broadly, but the English sequence
“buried” and then “burned” still suggests prior interment. The total-silence
proposal is rejected because the note uses “silence” rhetorically for the
supernatural calm and does not materially deny the crew's rowing noise. No
fixed-wording-only formula deviation is counted.

## Calibration Implications

Conjectural compass bearings must not be presented as Homeric route data.
Funeral vocabulary should be judged across the narrated rite, with severity
reduced when a broad ritual meaning remains available. Qualitative dimensions
must not be converted into exact modern units. Arithmetic and structural
interval claims require direct verification, and later chronology must be
checked before a note supplies a compressed forecast. Concrete nautical
hardware remains auditable. Conversely, rhetorical descriptions of supernatural
calm should not be forced into exceptionless claims about every incidental
sound.
