## Overall assessment

The passage is rendered with close control of agency, navigation, conditional
warnings, and the Sirens episode's repeated instructions. I found five firm
issues: one consequential funeral-sequence distortion, one concrete nautical
term displaced, and three localized but auditable explanatory-note
overstatements involving an invented measurement and two incorrect time or
line intervals.
