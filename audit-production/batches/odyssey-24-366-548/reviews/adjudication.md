# Adjudication — *Odyssey* 24.366–548

## Overall Assessment

The translation is strong and generally faithful. Eight distinct findings are sustained from ten reviewer proposals: six minor note or grammar errors and two moderate interpretive errors in the notes. The shared crew-count proposals are merged. R2-005 is rejected because “from of old” is an established English idiom, not a duplicated-preposition error.

All nine distinct proposal keys are resolved. No proposal is modified, uncertain, or unresolved, and no fixed-wording-only FORMULA deviation is counted.

## Calibration Implications

- Literary synthesis must not convert age cohorts into a genealogical sequence.
- Exact intervals and reconstructed totals require direct verification and explicit qualification.
- In coordinated groups, follow demonstratives and pronouns through the next action before assigning majority status.
- Preserve the interpretive range of rare political vocabulary such as `ἔκλησις`.
- Read adverbs with the prospective clause they govern.
- Do not flag established idioms as grammatical duplication.
