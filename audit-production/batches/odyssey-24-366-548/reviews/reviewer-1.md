# Reviewer 1

This is a strong, fluent, and generally precise rendering of the Odyssey’s closing movement. The verse preserves agency, sequence, and rhetorical force well. The firm problems are confined to explanatory-note overstatements: one misdescribes Melanthius’ punishment, one gives a false line interval, one presents an inferred crew total as textual fact, and two misstate the sequence or grammar of the final clash. No moderate, major, or critical defect was found.
