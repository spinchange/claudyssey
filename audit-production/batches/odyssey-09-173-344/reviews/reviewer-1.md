# Reviewer 1

## Overall assessment

The translation handles this demanding narrative sequence very well. The
Cyclops' isolation, the ordered dairy, Odysseus' formal supplication, the
abrupt cannibal violence, and the practical logic of the stake plan all remain
clear and forceful. The firm issues are localized: one concrete dairy term,
one weakening of the Cyclopes' claim of superior might, and two explanatory
claims that conflict with the narrated sequence or with the poem's assignment
of shipwrecks.

## Especially successful renderings

1. Lines 182-223 build the Cyclops' cave with impressive control, preserving
   both his landscape-like isolation and the careful age-sorting, vessels,
   whey, and repeated order of the dairy.
2. Lines 252-286 keep the full rhetorical progression from the inherited
   piracy question through Odysseus' appeal to Zeus of Strangers, the Cyclops'
   rejection, his probing ship-question, and Odysseus' protective lie.
3. Lines 287-305 render the first killings and Odysseus' checked sword-impulse
   with exact causal force: immediate revenge would leave the survivors
   entombed behind the stone.
