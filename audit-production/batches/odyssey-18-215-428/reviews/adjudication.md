## Rejected and Unresolved Proposals

R2-003 is rejected because “knees went slack” conventionally represents bodily support failing under `λύθεν ... γυῖα` without changing the event, and the note describes a broad collapse motif. R2-005 is rejected because the note immediately states that Eurymachus dies at the tables and never reaches the doors, making isolated “exact” ironic setup rather than an uncorrected factual claim. No proposal is unresolved.

Other concerns about tomorrow morning, loose formula terminology, a fatal necklace, Ithacan winter, and “breaks a servant” remain below threshold because context prevents material misunderstanding.

## Calibration Implications

Contextual plausibility does not license certain death from nonspecific harm. Closely related tools are not interchangeable when mechanics differ. Conventional body-part synecdoche is below threshold unless consequential. An inaccurate label may remain below threshold when the same note immediately supplies correct facts. Distinguish proposing an action from performing it, and do not invent exact elapsed hours.
