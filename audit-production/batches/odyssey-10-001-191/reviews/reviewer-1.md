# Reviewer 1

## Overall assessment

This is a strong translation of three sharply different movements: the
near-homecoming and reversal at Aeolia, the compact destruction at Telepylus,
and the exhausted arrival and recovery on Aeaea. Narrative agency, commands,
spatial mechanics, and the recurring first-person refrains are generally
preserved with unusual precision. I found one localized geographic
contradiction in the verse and two explanatory-note claims that overstate what
the narrative establishes about divine hostility and the lost fleet's size.

## Especially successful renderings

1. Lines 28-55 preserve the excruciating spatial and psychological logic of
   the Aeolian reversal: Ithaca's fires come into view, Odysseus finally
   sleeps, the crew's distributive grievance opens the bag, and his suicide
   deliberation resolves into silent endurance.
2. Lines 87-132 render the Laestrygonian harbor as both perfect shelter and
   perfect trap, keeping the narrow entrance, enclosed calm, ships moored
   together, cliff-top missiles, cut cable, urgent rowing order, and the lone
   escape causally legible.
3. Lines 156-182 sustain the stag episode's concrete hunting procedure—from
   the heat-driven descent to water through the spine-shot, withy rope,
   neck-carry, spear support, unveiling, and morale-restoring feast.
