## Rejected and Unresolved Proposals

One firm proposal was rejected. None remains unresolved.

### R2-005 — 20.382

`πολυκληΐς` describes a ship furnished with many tholes or rowlocks and therefore many oars. “Many-benched ship” uses the adjacent rowing station as a conventional functional metonym for the same large, many-oared vessel. It preserves the ship’s scale, propulsion, and role in the proposed voyage. The technical hardware distinction is real but does not reach the rubric’s materiality threshold.

The competing defenses of other disputed claims were also considered:

- At 20.349, the note’s language about the suitors experiencing their future partly restores the proleptic setting, but the verse still changes cognitive foreboding into desire. Because this is the hinge into the sustained prophetic vision, MODERATE severity is warranted.
- At note L364, Odysseus’ later declaration occurs at the attack’s opening and may no longer function as a warning in the ordinary preventive sense. Leodes’ earlier bow warning independently defeats the unrestricted claim.
- At note L387, “unseen” could figuratively mean unnoticed or unheeded, but the note does not qualify it that way, and the source positively places Penelope opposite the men.

## Calibration Implications

- Broad and split reviewer proposals should be normalized to shared semantic keys while preserving a one-to-one mapping between firm review IDs and proposal-map entries.
- A secondary component may support another finding contextually without creating a duplicate map entry for the same review ID.
- Book boundaries do not create elapsed days; notes must track explicit dawns and nights through Books 20–22.
- “Last,” “first,” and “before any was born” require a complete and securely defined comparison class.
- Exact measurements may not be inferred merely from characters sharing a room.
- Desire, expectation, and foreboding are distinct mental states, especially in prophetic narration.
- Lunar symbolism does not license presenting supernatural solar darkness as ordinary astronomy.
- Conventional technical metonymy remains below threshold when scale, function, and action are preserved.
- An opposite-facing audience should not be called unseen without explicit concealment or a clearly marked figurative sense.
