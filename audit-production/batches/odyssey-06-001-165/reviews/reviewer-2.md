## Especially Successful Renderings

- **6.25–40:** Athena’s dream-speech accurately preserves its indirect wedding logic, balancing laundry, reputation, family honor, and practical transport without prematurely mentioning Odysseus.
- **6.102–109:** The Artemis simile retains its full structure: the mountain range, boars and swift deer, accompanying nymphs, Leto’s delight, and Nausicaa’s recognizable preeminence.
- **6.130–148:** The lion simile and subsequent deliberation preserve both physical desperation and tactical intelligence, including hunger’s command, Nausicaa’s terror, and Odysseus’ choice of distanced verbal supplication.
