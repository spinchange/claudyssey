## Especially Successful Renderings

1. **24.193–202:** Agamemnon’s paired songs of Penelope and Clytemnestra preserve the praise, misogynistic generalization, and antithetical structure of the Greek.
2. **24.280–314:** Laertes’ hospitality response and Odysseus’ final false identity retain the xenia logic, precise gift exchange, invented genealogy, dating, and favorable omens.
3. **24.331–348:** The scar and orchard-tree tokens are rendered clearly and concretely, carrying the recognition from remembered wound to countable childhood inheritance.
