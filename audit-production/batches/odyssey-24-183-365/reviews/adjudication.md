# Adjudication — *Odyssey* 24.183–365

## Overall Assessment

The translation is highly faithful across Agamemnon’s praise of Penelope, Laertes’ physical portrait, Odysseus’ final false identity, and the orchard recognition. The eight sustained findings are all minor: one localized lexical weakening and seven explanatory-note errors involving numerical chronology, recurrence history, unsupported family patterns, disguise duration, or speaker attribution.

Twelve firm reviewer IDs resolve to eight distinct proposal keys. Four pairs were merged, including the conflicting proposals at 24.289. The correct recurrence speakers there are Telemachus at 15.268, Penelope at 19.315, and Laertes at 24.289. R1 correctly identifies the note’s erroneous triad; R2 correctly challenges “husband” but incorrectly assigns 19.315 to disguised Odysseus.

Adjudication status: COMPLETE. All eight keys are sustained, though R2-004 is modified to the actual error. Nothing remains unresolved. No fixed-wording-only FORMULA deviation was counted.
