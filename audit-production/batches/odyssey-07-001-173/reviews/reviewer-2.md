# Reviewer 2 Report

The translation is semantically strong across the batch, preserving the movement through Scheria, the palace description, and the staged supplication. I found one firm localized lexical distortion: Laodamas' heroic epithet is replaced with an unrelated characterization of kindness.

| ID | Lines | Category | Severity | Confidence | Finding | Greek evidence | Suggested direction |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| R2-001 | 7.170 | LEXICAL | MINOR | 0.95 | "Kind-hearted Laodamas" replaces the heroic characterization in `ἀγαπήνορα` with an unsupported benevolent temperament. The epithet marks Laodamas as manly, gallant, or heroically vigorous, not kind-hearted. | `υἱὸν ἀναστήσας ἀγαπήνορα Λαοδάμαντα` | Use "gallant Laodamas," "manly Laodamas," or another compact rendering that preserves heroic vigor. |

## Especially Successful Renderings

1. **7.14-17:** Athena's protective mist and its purpose are clearly preserved.
2. **7.117-131:** The perpetual orchard and vineyard retain the simultaneous stages of growth, harvesting, treading, ripening, and the two springs.

| 7.71-72 | 0.64 | "Greet her with blessings" specifies blessings where `δειδέχαται μύθοισιν` says that the people greet or welcome her with words. | Her exceptional public honor is explicit, and the added specificity has little independent effect. |
