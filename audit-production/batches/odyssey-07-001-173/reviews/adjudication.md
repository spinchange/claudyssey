## Calibration Implications

Parse compact English syntax fully before treating apparent ellipsis as semantic displacement. At 7.99, "what they had" is a viable free-relative subject, so the clause retains the source's unfailing supply. Conversely, a specific heroic epithet remains auditable when English substitutes an unrelated moral quality; nearby affection does not compensate for lost heroic characterization.
