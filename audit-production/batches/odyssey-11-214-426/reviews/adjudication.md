# Adjudication Report

Reviewer 1 proposed two findings and Reviewer 2 proposed two. All four are
distinct and sustained. At 11.297, “decrees of fate” replaces Melampus'
prophetic or divine utterances with an unexpressed abstraction. The 11.263 note
turns Amphion and Zethus' priority in establishing and walling seven-gated
Thebes into an unsupported claim that their foundation preceded Cadmus. At
11.303-304, the English makes Castor and Polydeuces alternate with one another,
although the plural Greek predicates make the brothers live and die together
on alternate days. At 11.401-403, “carved away” changes a hypothetical cattle
raid into the carving or slaughter of livestock. No fixed-wording-only formula
deviation is counted.

- **11.218-224:** Anticleia's account moves precisely from bodily dissolution

  Odysseus remember the scene for Penelope.

  emotionally forceful.

## Calibration Implications

Prophetic vocabulary should preserve the divine or oracular character of an
utterance rather than substituting an unexpressed abstraction such as Fate.
Interpretive notes must not turn a local priority claim into an unsupported
chronology that resolves compressed or competing mythic traditions. With
coordinated plural subjects, predicates must be tested together before English
distributive wording such as “each in turn” is introduced. Finally, concrete
raid vocabulary should distinguish cutting livestock off from a herd from
cutting up the animals themselves.
