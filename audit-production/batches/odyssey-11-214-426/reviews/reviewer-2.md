# Reviewer 2

## Overall assessment

This is a strong and generally exact rendering of a varied passage, especially
in the death-physiology speech, the catalogue's compressed genealogies, and the
return to Agamemnon. I found two material local deviations: the Dioscuri's
shared alternate-day condition is recast as an alternation between the two
brothers, and a hypothetical cattle raid is turned into the concrete but
different action of carving cattle.

| ID | Lines | Category | Severity | Confidence | Finding | Greek evidence | Suggested direction |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| R2-001 | 11.303-304 | GRAMMAR | MODERATE | 0.93 | “One day each in turn” makes Castor and Polydeuces alternate with one another, one brother living while the other is dead. The Greek gives the two brothers plural predicates and says that together they live every other day and together are dead at other times. This changes the nature of their exceptional shared afterlife. | `ἄλλοτε μὲν ζώουσʼ ἑτερήμεροι, ἄλλοτε δʼ αὖτε / τεθνᾶσιν` has plural `ζώουσι` and `τεθνᾶσιν` with the joint subject `οἳ`; `ἑτερήμεροι` means “every other day,” not “one brother each day.” | Render the brothers' condition jointly: “on alternate days they live, and on alternate days again / they are dead.” |
| R2-002 | 11.401-403 | MISTRANSLATION | MINOR | 0.87 | “As you carved away their cattle” replaces the hypothetical action of cutting off, rounding up, or raiding cattle with carving/slaughtering them. The surrounding alternatives concern death during a land raid or a fight over a city and women, so the changed concrete action is misleading. | `βοῦς περιταμνόμενον ἠδʼ οἰῶν πώεα καλά` describes cutting off or carrying off cattle and fine flocks; it does not describe carving their flesh. | Use “as you rounded up/drove off their cattle and fine flocks of sheep.” |

## Especially successful renderings

1. **11.218-224** clearly preserves Anticleia's sequence from bodily dissolution
   through the dreamlike soul's flight and her injunction to remember the scene
   for Penelope.
2. **11.271-280** handles the dense Oedipus/Epicaste summary with its agency,
   chronology, continued rule, suicide, and avenging Furies all intact.
3. **11.308-320** lucidly carries over the Aloadae's exact dimensions, threat,
   counterfactual achievement, killer, and prepubescent death.
