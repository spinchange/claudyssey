# Reviewer 1 — Odyssey 11.214–426

## Overall assessment

The passage is consistently strong in its handling of the catalogue, the Phaeacian intermezzo, and Agamemnon's entrance. Two localized problems cross the reporting threshold: an oracular utterance is recast as an impersonal decree of fate, and a note turns Amphion and Zethus' priority within the Theban foundation story into an unsupported priority over Cadmus. Several more aggressive poetic choices remain defensible or concern genuinely disputed Greek and are therefore not counted. Fixed-register wording alone has not been counted.

## Especially successful renderings

1. **11.218–224** clearly carries Anticleia's explanation through sinews, fire, departing life, and the dreamlike soul while preserving the consolatory turn toward the living wife.
2. **11.301–304** lucidly preserves the Dioscuri's exceptional alternating life and death and their honor equal to the gods.
3. **11.387–394** gives Agamemnon's grieving approach real force while accurately retaining the shade's lost bodily strength and failed reach.
