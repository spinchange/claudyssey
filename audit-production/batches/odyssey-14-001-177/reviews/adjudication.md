# Adjudication Report

The translation is highly faithful in narrative sequence, agency, speech, and
characterization. Six distinct findings are sustained from the reviewers'
nine firm proposals: one `MODERATE` reversal of Eumaeus' evidential reasoning
and five `MINOR` local deviations, four of them in explanatory notes. The two
reviewers independently identified the ground-sleeping sows and the unsupported
five-year age, so each pair is merged. The proposal concerning “starving for
bread” remains plausible but below the firm materiality threshold. No
fixed-wording-only `FORMULA` deviation is counted.

## Calibration Implications

Explanatory-note rhetoric remains auditable when it turns a plausible social
inference into an exact duration, compensation claim, exclusive possession, or
deliberate action. Concrete animal-husbandry vocabulary should be preserved
when it specifies housing rather than merely decorating the scene. Reviewers
should also test causal and evidential connectives in the full argument:
retaining both propositions does not compensate for reversing which one
supports the other. At the same time, ordinary intensification and food
synecdoche should remain below threshold when context makes them defensible and
their material consequence is uncertain.
