## Especially Successful Renderings

- **14.193-228:** The performance preamble and false Cretan's social and
  martial autobiography preserve their syntax, characterization, and contrast

  sequence and xenia logic.
- **14.301-315:** The Book 12 shipwreck material preserves the cloud,
  thunderbolt, sulfur, sea-crow simile, saving mast, and exact nine-day and
  tenth-night chronology without a distinct semantic formula failure.

## Calibration Implications

Concrete acquisition actions should not be reduced to emotional responses when
the surrounding contrast depends on how property was obtained. As calibrated
at 12.397-399, point-in-time English must not replace Greek duration when the
next numbered day controls the sequence. Notes should not add exact durations,
lodging, names, or offices from plausible context alone. Claims that a fiction
is assembled from truth must distinguish thematic transposition from false
itinerary and location, and relative-day claims must be checked against
continuous book-to-book chronology.
