## Especially Successful Renderings

- **8.403-405:** The sword's bronze, silver hilt, fresh-cut ivory scabbard,
  and high value are all preserved through a dense gift description.
- **8.461-468:** Nausicaa's `ζωάγρια` is forcefully retained as "the price of
  your life," and Odysseus' reply preserves both godlike honor and his direct
  acknowledgment that she saved him.
- **8.523-531:** The captive-widow simile keeps its full sequence: defensive
  death, bodily lament, blows to back and shoulders, enslavement, wasting
  grief, and the return to Odysseus' tears.

## Calibration Implications

Numerical claims about cross-book spacing should identify the endpoint and be
checked directly. A note must not convert `ἄλλου ἀκούσας`, received report,
into a one-link eyewitness rule without further source support. Claims that a
recurrence is verbatim remain auditable note assertions when the Greek itself
changes a meaningful category; this is distinct from an automated
fixed-English-wording deviation. Conversely, an English summons that names the
required action can preserve operative speech force without using "bid" or
"command."
