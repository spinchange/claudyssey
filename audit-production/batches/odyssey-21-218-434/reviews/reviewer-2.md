# Reviewer 2 — Semantic Review

The verse translation is highly controlled and generally preserves action, agency, and dramatic pacing. The firm issues are overwhelmingly in the notes, where exact durations, distances, counts, positions, or cross-book claims are sometimes asserted without support. One verse passage also collapses a social-propriety judgment into mere improbability. No fixed-wording-only formula deviations are counted.

## Especially Successful Renderings

1. **21.218–241:** The scar recognition, reciprocal embrace, interrupted weeping, and operational orders are rendered with clear changes of agency and persuasive emotional pacing.
2. **21.293–310:** Antinous’ Centaur exemplum preserves the causal sequence from intoxication through mutilation, feud, and threat while maintaining his aggressive voice.
3. **21.405–423:** The lyre simile, effortless stringing, swallow-like note, divine omen, seated shot, and passage through the axes form an exceptionally lucid and forceful climax.
