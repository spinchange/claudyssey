# Adjudication — *Odyssey* 21.218–434

## Overall Assessment

The verse translation is consistently strong through the recognition, debate, handoff, bow-stringing, and shot. The sustained problems are concentrated in explanatory notes that convert interpretation into exact history, measurement, recurrence, agency, or universal claims. One verse passage loses a distinct judgment of social fitness.

Seventeen distinct findings are sustained from twenty-six firm reviewer proposals. Eight true duplicate pairs were merged, producing eighteen distinct proposal keys. One proposal, R1-011, is rejected because the surrounding thief-language preserves the hostile accusation and “schooled in trouble” remains capable of an active troublemaking sense.

The Eumaeus locking proposal is sustained as GRAMMAR and MODERATE: the note transfers a strategically consequential act from Eurycleia to Eumaeus and builds its occupational scheme on that false agency. The “first note in twenty years” concern is promoted to a firm MINOR ADDITION because prolonged disuse is plausible but a complete twenty-year history is not stated.

Adjudication status: COMPLETE. No firm proposal remains uncertain or unresolved. No fixed-wording-only FORMULA issue was counted.

## Calibration Implications

- A note may not transfer a consequential action merely because one character relays an order another performs.
- Exact times, distances, headcounts, and complete object histories require textual support.
- Repeated lines do not license universals about every recognition scene, speaker, or taunt.
- Social-propriety `ἔοικεν` should remain distinct from probability when reputation drives the speech.
- “Verbatim” requires actual verbal recurrence.
- False explanatory recurrence counts remain semantic-note findings rather than fixed-register FORMULA findings.
- A reused gesture does not enact an earlier signal when the earlier instruction is complete and the new action differs.
