# Reviewer 1 — Odyssey 21.218–434

The verse translation is consistently strong, particularly in the recognition, debate, and bow-stringing sequences. Most firm issues occur in the explanatory notes, where vivid interpretation sometimes becomes incorrect chronology, recurrence, agency, measurement, or universal claims. Two localized verse problems remain.

## Especially Successful Renderings

1. Lines 218–241 clearly preserve the scar recognition, the reciprocal embrace, the counterfactual sunset, and Odysseus’ carefully divided instructions concerning bow, women, and courtyard gates.
2. Lines 249–280 handle three changing rhetorical positions well: Eurymachus’ shame, Antinous’ religious pretext for postponement, and Odysseus’ strategic plea to test his supposedly diminished strength.
3. Lines 404–434 render the climax with impressive precision: the lyre simile, effortless stringing, swallow-like note, divine omen, seated shot through the axe sockets, coded feast language, and Telemachus’ final arming all remain coherent.

| 21.267 | 0.61 | “On the altar” supplies an unexpressed physical location for the sacrificial thigh-pieces. | An altar or sacrificial fire is conventional and implicit in offering thigh-pieces to Apollo, so the added staging has little material effect. |
| 21.289 | 0.68 | “The great ones” softens self-descriptive `ὑπερφιάλοισι`. | As arrogant elite self-presentation, “great ones” can convey the boast from the speaker’s perspective. |
| 21.295 note | 0.68 | Calling Eurytion’s mutilation Melanthius’ “exact fate” suppresses differences in location and additional mutilation. | The note immediately defines the likeness broadly, so “exact” may be rhetorical structural emphasis. |
| 21.344–345 | 0.65 | “No Achaean has better right than I” converts superior strength or authority into normative entitlement. | “Better right” can idiomatically express superior household authority, made explicit by the following clause. |
| 21.411 note | 0.62 | “The bow’s first note in twenty years” assumes complete non-use throughout Odysseus’ absence. | Long storage, Telemachus’ unfamiliarity, and everyone’s inability to string it make prolonged disuse a strong contextual inference. |
