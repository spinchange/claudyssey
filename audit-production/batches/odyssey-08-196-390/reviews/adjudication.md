## Calibration Implications

Omitted negation in a boast is moderate when it changes exclusive superiority to parity. Preserve technical civic roles such as judges or umpires. Transferring an evaluative adjective into a nearby visual effect remains a minor lexical loss. Rhetorical shorthand is below threshold when a note itself states the allegedly omitted facts.
