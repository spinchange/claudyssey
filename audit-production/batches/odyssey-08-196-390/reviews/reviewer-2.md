# Reviewer 2 Report

The translation is highly faithful across *Odyssey* 8.196-390, preserving the athletic boast, Demodocus' comic structure, and the bargaining that releases Ares. I find two localized lexical losses in the preparation of the dance.

| ID | Lines | Category | Severity | Confidence | Finding | Greek evidence | Suggested direction |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| R2-001 | 8.258-260 | LEXICAL | MINOR | 0.86 | "Chosen stewards" preserves selected public officials but loses their specific role as judges or umpires of the contests. Their work preparing the performance space does not erase the institutional title. | `αἰσυμνῆται δὲ κριτοὶ ἐννέα ... δήμιοι`: `κριτοί` marks selection, while `αἰσυμνῆται` identifies officials who judge or arbitrate. | Use "nine chosen judges" or "nine appointed umpires." |
| R2-002 | 8.263-265 | LEXICAL | MINOR | 0.80 | "The shining floor" replaces `χορὸν θεῖον`, the divine or wondrous dance floor or dance, with an unsupported visual property. The following flickering feet do not restore the evaluative elevation. | `πέπληγον δὲ χορὸν θεῖον ποσίν`: `θεῖον` marks the `χορός` as divine, wondrous, or surpassingly excellent, not literally shining. | Use "the wondrous floor," "the divine dance floor," or "their wondrous dance." |

## Especially Successful Renderings

1. **8.215-233:** The bow boast preserves its hierarchy, Eurytus' death, spear comparison, and bodily concession about running.
2. **8.266-332:** The adultery song retains secrecy, surveillance, manufacture of the trap, immobilization, exposure, and proverb.
