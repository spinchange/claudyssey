## Calibration Implications

Concrete literary parallels in notes must be verified at the level of the asserted action: the presence of a distaff elsewhere does not establish a repeated falling gesture. Decorative expansion remains auditable when it adds a specific cultural feature to a merely spatial epithet. Pronouns in compressed genealogies must preserve successive generations when the passage's argument depends on isolation through only-son descent. Cross-book structural comparisons must distinguish participants who occupy different kinship roles. Finally, the 14.321-330 anchor applies when commentary treats the fictional Thesprotian treasure and itinerary as the same facts as the real Phaeacian return; preserving a broader honesty contrast does not repair that factual equivalence. No fixed-register wording difference produces a separate finding here.
