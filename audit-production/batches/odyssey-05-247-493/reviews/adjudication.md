# Adjudication Report

The translation is consistently faithful through the raft-building, storm, landfall, and recovery sequence. Four localized findings survive adjudication: an unsupported technical purpose for the brushwood, substitution of rollers for explicit levers, weakening of Poseidon's hostility, and reassignment of Sleep's closing agency to Athena. The proposed common-root error at 5.477 and note L477 is rejected because `ἐξ ὁμόθεν πεφυῶτας` permits a common-source or common-growth reading broad enough to support “single stock”; “from the same spot” is safer but not uniquely required. No formula-register-only issue is counted.
