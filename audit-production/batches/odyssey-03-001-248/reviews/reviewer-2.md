| R2-001 | 3.84-85 | GRAMMAR | MODERATE | 0.94 | "fought at your side when you sacked the Trojans' city" makes Nestor the grammatical agent of the sack and leaves Odysseus merely fighting alongside him. ## Especially Successful Renderings

- **3.92-101:** Telemachus' supplication is clear and socially exact; "Do not soften anything from courtesy or pity" gives `μηδέ ... μειλίσσεο` strong idiomatic force while the surrounding lines preserve the appeal at Nestor's knees and the request for eyewitness truth.
- **3.118-119:** "Nine years we stitched evils around them" preserves the concrete sewing metaphor of `κακὰ ῥάπτομεν` without losing the military plotting that the metaphor expresses.
- **3.159-175:** The departure from Tenedos and route decision at Lesbos remain unusually lucid despite difficult geography and syntax; "aching for home" is particularly effective for `οἴκαδε ἱέμενοι`, and the divine sign, open-sea crossing, and purpose clause all retain their relations.
