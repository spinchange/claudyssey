## Especially Successful Renderings

- **16.241-269:** Telemachus' numerical reckoning and the exchange about Athena and Zeus preserve the impossible scale of the proposed fight, the exact regional head count, and the dry contrast between youthful caution and Odysseus' confidence.
- **16.321-341:** The arrival of Telemachus' ship and the two messengers' contrasting delivery styles remain clear, preserving both the public herald's announcement and Eumaeus' private fulfillment of Telemachus' commission.
- **16.418-448:** Penelope's indictment and Eurymachus' deceitful reassurance retain their opposed rhetorical structures—family debt, violated hospitality, false protection, remembered childhood intimacy, and the narrator's immediate exposure of the lie.

## Calibration Implications

Repeated verbs must be read with their changing objects and narrative sequence: after weapons are taken, a second `ἑλοίμεθα` can advance to taking the opponents rather than repeat the first action. Speech-act labels remain auditable when commentary upgrades assertion into oath. Explicit English numerals require direct Greek support, though nearby corrective context can reduce severity. Priority claims about a character's role must be checked against earlier books, and universal structural claims remain reportable when a clear counterexample defeats them despite a valid narrower pattern. Rhetorical characterization, idiomatic intensification, and plausible topographical interpretation should remain below threshold when they do not materially alter action or narrative mechanics. No fixed-register wording difference produces a separate finding here.
