# Reviewer 2

The verse translation is exceptionally controlled through the supplications, moral reckoning, cleanup, executions, purification, and recognition. Firm issues are confined to explanatory notes that miscount recurrences or decisions, impose unsupported chronology or causal balance, or inaccurately summarize earlier scenes. No fixed-wording-only formula deviations are counted.

## Especially Successful Renderings

1. **22.335–380:** Phemius’ deliberation and plea, Telemachus’ intervention, Medon’s emergence from the oxhide, and Odysseus’ moral maxim preserve both the comedy and the genuine danger.
2. **22.401–418:** The blood-fed lion simile, Eurycleia’s attempted victory cry, and Odysseus’ prohibition against exulting over the dead are rendered with precise shifts of action and ethical register.
3. **22.447–501:** The forced cleanup, hangings, mutilation, sulfur fumigation, and final embrace by the loyal women retain the Greek’s concrete sequencing without euphemizing its violence.
