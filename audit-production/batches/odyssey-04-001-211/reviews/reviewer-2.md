# Reviewer 2

## Overall Assessment

This is a strong, attentive rendering that preserves the passage’s hospitality scenes, recognition sequence, and emotional progression with unusual precision. Three firm issues remain: one localized transfer of an epithet, one materially false explanatory-note claim, and one weakening of Peisistratus’ operative request. No formula-register-only deviations are counted.

## Especially Successful Renderings

1. **Lines 49–56:** The hospitality type-scene is exact and lucid, preserving the distinctions among `δμῳαί`, `ἀμφίπολος`, and `ταμίη` while carrying the bathing, handwashing, table, and provisions cleanly across the lineation.
2. **Lines 71–75:** Telemachus’ wonder is particularly successful: “lightning of bronze,” the full material catalogue, “past speaking,” and “Awe takes me” preserve both sensory splendor and `σέβας`.
3. **Lines 100–112:** Menelaus’ movement from grief for all the dead to his singular, unforgetting grief for Odysseus remains semantically controlled and emotionally forceful, including the loss of sleep and food and the uncertainty over whether Odysseus lives.
