# Reviewer 1

Overall, the translation is highly effective and generally faithful, with especially strong handling of Penelope’s skepticism, the false-wedding strategy, and Odysseus’ beautification. I found one moderate grammatical distortion and five minor verse-or-note issues. None involves fixed-register wording alone.

## Especially Successful Renderings

1. **23.35–38:** Penelope’s demand to know how one man overcame the suitors preserves both her evidentiary skepticism and the force of `μοῦνος ... ἀολλέες`.
2. **23.105–110:** Her stunned inability to speak or look, followed by the claim of mutually known hidden signs, is lucid and closely tracks the Greek progression.
3. **23.156–162:** Athena’s beautification and the gold-over-silver craftsman simile retain the imagery, agency, and rhetorical architecture of the Greek with exceptional precision.
