## Calibration Implications

Cross-references in notes must be tested against the cited passages, especially when they universalize an ethnographic characterization. Commercial nouns and verbal context must preserve buyer/seller direction. A note may identify a genuine thematic motif yet still make a minor auditable error by naming an absent Greek formula; this is distinct from a fixed-register English wording variation. Prose continuity across a book boundary should not be described as syntactic incompletion unless the clauses actually require it.
