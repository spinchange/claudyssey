### odyssey-01-001-155

Detailed adjudication: [audit-poc/reviews/adjudication.md](../audit-poc/reviews/adjudication.md)

Two independent reviewers and one adjudicator assessed *Odyssey* 1.1-155.
The adjudicator characterized the translation as highly faithful and sustained
three localized `MODERATE` findings. There were no `MAJOR` or `CRITICAL`
findings.

| Lines | Result |
|---|---|
| 1.58-59 | “Would be glad to see” weakens the force of `ἱέμενος`, Odysseus' intense longing even to see Ithaca's smoke. |
| 1.62 | “At odds with Odysseus” preserves the pun but weakens `ὠδύσαο`, strong anger or hostility. |
| 1.90-92 | “Speak out to all the suitors” loses the prohibitive force of `ἀπειπέμεν`: Telemachus is to warn off or forbid them. |

Reviewer 2 also questioned “all evils” at 1.33. The adjudicator left this
unresolved and excluded it from the error count because a bare Greek plural can
support a generic reading.
