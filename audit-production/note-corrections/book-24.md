**Current** (`translation/book-24.md:680`):

**Proposed:**
> [^L477]: The settlement's terms: oaths cut, Odysseus king "all his days," and — the extraordinary clause — "let us set a forgetting of the killing of sons and of brothers" (ἔκλησιν θέωμεν): whether a literal blotting of memory or an amnesty in the word's oldest sense, a laying-down of the grievance, the text does not say. **Why:** ἔκλησις ranges from memory-erasure to cessation of grievance; the note collapsed it to the supernatural mechanism. Preserving the range keeps the meditation intact — arguably deepens it.
